14 November 2026The depreciation rates calculator now takes a taxable use percentage and shows prime cost and diminishing value side by side, and a drawn schedule can be copied as a citation line or a link that restores it, downloaded as CSV or printed. The page names the 2025 determination as LI 2025/20, says that TR 2022/1 was withdrawn with effect from 31 October 2025, and adds common questions, a note of its changes, a correction link and Dataset structured data for its JSON file.
14 November 2026A new depreciation rates page searches and lists the 7,430 effective lives in the Commissioner of Taxation’s 2025 determination, registered on 15 September 2025 and read on 13 November 2026. Each row shows the life, the prime cost and diminishing value rates it gives and the date it was last set, and a calculator works out a year’s decline in value from a cost, a life and a start date. The tables are also published as a JSON file with their source and the date it was read.
14 November 2026The profit and cash flow case and the release record name au-fpa-pack v0.5.0, published on 13 November, as the current release. The case stays pinned to commit 386c7ff, which produced its workbook and figures.
14 November 2026A new Payday Super business days page lists the 17 weekdays from 1 July 2026 to 31 August 2027 that are public holidays for the whole of a state or territory, and so not business days for any employer, with a CSV and the official source for each jurisdiction, checked on 14 November 2026.
13 November 2026The GST, cash forecast, margin, break-even, hourly pricing and Division 7A calculators now show a Last checked date of 13 November 2026, the day their sources were reread against their rates and definitions; the 4 calculators that cite no external source keep their 27 September 2026 review date. The margin, break-even and hourly pricing calculators cited a business.gov.au pricing page that now redirects to one without those definitions, so they now cite Business Queensland’s break-even and pricing guidance. The calculators page shows the newest check. The site checks fail if a calculator’s Last checked date is not the newest source read in its Sources applied list, is older than its official comparison or newer than its dateModified, or if its Sources applied list states a source read without a Last checked date.
13 November 2026Each of the 10 business calculators now opens with a fictional example already worked: its first boundary case fills the inputs and its result shows on arrival, labelled as an example. The first edit clears it.

Business Queensland ignored the cents in amounts entered, which is why the inputs are whole dollars. No calculation changed.
13 November 2026A Division 7A minimum yearly repayment calculator works out, for one amalgamated loan and one income year, the repayment the formula in subsection 109E(6) of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 requires, with its interest and principal, at that year’s rate from this site’s benchmark rate table. It matches the ATO’s worked example and the published fixtures of the div7a-loan-review rule, and says what it does not cover.

Worked examples stay pinned to the releases they were run against.

It also gives the free Starter developer plan’s limit of 5 organisations at 1,000 API calls a day each and Xero’s exemption for practice integrations. The trial balance exporter is unchanged.

Apart from the Privacy crawler summary no visible text changed, and no rate, calculation or release changed.
