What changed and when, across this site and the tagged tool releases. Dates are Australian Eastern time.

DateChange
22 September 2026The car limit page now explains which GST credits reduce the cost. Division 7A figures are qualified for companies with a 30 June year end, with guidance for substituted accounting periods. Workpaper Review Gate describes its released output safeguard. The machine index, tool source dates, financial year labels and Newcastle spelling were corrected, and generated text copies were refreshed.
22 September 2026The homepage now leads with Open source tools for Australian accountants and gives clearer starting points for accounting tasks. Mobile links gained larger touch targets and layouts support enlarged text. Pure dark mode remains, with brighter homepage text, a smaller headline, sentence-case labels and simpler dividers. Unnecessary hyphens were removed across the site, stylesheet URLs were refreshed, and all five messaging preview cards were updated.
20 September 2026Actions from the 20 September GEO audit: Evaluations joined the primary navigation; the Privacy page states which AI crawlers may fetch and cite and which training crawlers are disallowed; llm-tax-guardrails is labelled an external GitHub guide, not a site tool, on the refusals page and in the machine-readable index; the index review date moved to 20 September and lists the Atom feed, which the homepage now advertises.
20 September 2026The 100 accounting questions moved to ten topic pages and the nine business calculators to one page each, with the old addresses kept as indexes. Three rates tables added: the maximum super contribution base, the car limit for depreciation, and the FBT rate and gross-up rates. The homepage scope strip and the Tools category links were removed, six page titles lost the site-name suffix, breadcrumbs reached every page, secondary text and form controls grew on small screens, and an Atom feed of this changelog was added.
20 September 2026Actions from the 19 September site audits: the Coal LSL calculator now takes a reporting month on every branch, refuses months before July 2023 and exports the exact wage basis and period in its CSV; the Payday and ATO benchmark pages carry their matching-quality and missing-bucket warnings beside the download steps; installation routes are labelled released, pinned or development; the evaluation email copy, franking introduction, cents-per-kilometre and WIP scope notes, privacy wording, machine index super summary, README tool count and changelog caption were corrected; Ozzit joined the evaluation chooser.
14 September 2026The six webfonts return to IBM's unmodified Latin1 files, the response header notes separate the GitHub Pages origin from the Cloudflare edge, the cash forecast names the dates it accepts, and the Coal LSL page explains the incorporated base pay definition.
11 September 2026Updated MCP capabilities and current release references, added verified trust and company tax examples, and widened the refusal tables for mobile reading.
6 September 2026The Tools page gained a Related resources section holding the third-party libraries, official guidance, and primary sources from the separate index repository, which is now archived; the footer links there instead.
4 September 2026Every link to a repository archived after the September 2026 consolidation now points at its maintained home in the monorepos, the 3 evaluations were rerun against the namespaced monorepo releases, and the site checks fail on any link that resolves to an archived repository.
2 September 2026Copy edit across the tool, rate, and evaluation pages, About, Contact, and Evidence: duplicated paragraphs removed, stray adverbs and rhetorical contrasts cut, disclaimers and bylines put in the active voice, and the super guarantee page moved to the site's per cent style.
2 September 2026Font preloads and a Content Security Policy on every page, entry headings on the Rates and Evaluations hubs, a no-script notice on the Coal LSL calculator, a security.txt contact file, and repository tooling removed from the published origin.
