# Changelog

What changed and when, across this site and the tagged tool releases. Dates are Australian Eastern time.

## Site

Date | Change
20 September 2026 | Actions from the 19 September site audits: the Coal LSL calculator now takes a reporting month on every branch, refuses months before July 2023 and exports the exact wage basis and period in its CSV; the Payday and ATO benchmark pages carry their matching-quality and missing-bucket warnings beside the download steps; installation routes are labelled released, pinned or development; the evaluation email copy, franking introduction, cents-per-kilometre and WIP scope notes, privacy wording, machine-index super summary, README tool count and changelog caption were corrected; Ozzit joined the evaluation chooser.
14 September 2026 | The six webfonts return to IBM's unmodified Latin1 files, the response-header notes separate the GitHub Pages origin from the Cloudflare edge, the cash forecast names the dates it accepts, and the Coal LSL page explains the incorporated base-pay definition.
11 September 2026 | Updated MCP capabilities and current release references, added verified trust and company-tax examples, and widened the refusal tables for mobile reading.
6 September 2026 | The Tools page gained a Related resources section holding the third-party libraries, official guidance, and primary sources from the separate index repository, which is now archived; the footer links there instead.
4 September 2026 | Every link to a repository archived after the September 2026 consolidation now points at its maintained home in the monorepos, the 3 evaluations were rerun against the namespaced monorepo releases, and the site checks fail on any link that resolves to an archived repository.
2 September 2026 | Copy edit across the tool, rate, and evaluation pages, About, Contact, and Evidence: duplicated paragraphs removed, stray adverbs and rhetorical contrasts cut, disclaimers and bylines put in the active voice, and the super guarantee page moved to the site's per cent style.
2 September 2026 | Font preloads and a Content Security Policy on every page, entry headings on the Rates and Evaluations hubs, a no-script notice on the Coal LSL calculator, a security.txt contact file, and repository tooling removed from the published origin.
31 August 2026 | Engage parked. The site remains a personal open-source tool index, not a practice, and is not accepting professional engagements through this site.
30 August 2026 | Navigation, adoption, and accessibility refinements across the register, with contact and changelog pages added.
30 August 2026 | Coal LSL calculator month validation and print recalculation fixes, and the split 2013-14 super guarantee history row.
29 August 2026 | Adoption-first homepage, the Tools, Rates, and Evaluations hubs, contextual share cards, and calculator print and CSV improvements.
28 August 2026 | True-black register redesign, self-hosted IBM Plex subsets, and the register-seal site icon.
