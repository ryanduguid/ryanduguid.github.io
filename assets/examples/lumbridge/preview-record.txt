Captured 24 September 2026 with desktop Excel 16.0 on Windows.
The source workbook was opened read-only with links disabled and macros
disabled. Assumptions!B2 was set to 45 in memory and CalculateFullRebuild ran.
The first chart on Cash 13 weeks stores a fixed layout that Excel's object
model cannot reposition, so a line chart was added in memory to the same
sheet from that chart's two SERIES formulas:
=SERIES('Cash 13 weeks'!$F$1,'Cash 13 weeks'!$B$2:$B$14,'Cash 13 weeks'!$F$2:$F$14,1)
=SERIES('Cash 13 weeks'!$G$1,'Cash 13 weeks'!$B$2:$B$14,'Cash 13 weeks'!$G$2:$G$14,2)
Excel reads every plotted date and value from those workbook cells.

Formatting applied in memory for the website's true-black canvas: chart fill
#000000 with a #262626 border; horizontal gridlines only; the category axis
line drawn at zero; AUD tick labels in $20,000 steps, with the value axis
starting one step below the step under the lowest week, to leave room for
its label; the date axis extended three weeks past 31 December with those
extra labels blanked, to leave room for the end labels; the legend replaced
by direct labels for the lowest week, the final closing cash and the buffer;
IBM Plex Sans text from the same v6.4.2 family the site serves, installed for
the exporting user so the chart matches the page. No value, date or series
was altered.

Two variants were exported with Excel Chart.Export as PNG at this display
scale. Their text is sized so the labels render at about 12px or more where
the pages show them.
- cash-preview.png: 957 by 479.5 points, 1282 by 639 pixels; 22 point text
  and title; fortnightly date labels. It is the full-size link and serves
  wide hero columns and the stacked tablet layout.
- cash-preview-narrow.png: 640 by 533.5 points, 856 by 711 pixels; 24 point
  text and title; four-weekly date labels. It serves phones and the tighter
  two-column widths between 56 and 75rem.
The source workbook was closed without saving; its hash remained unchanged.
