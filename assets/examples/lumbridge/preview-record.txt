Captured 13 September 2026 with desktop Excel 16.0 on Windows.
The source workbook was opened read-only with links disabled and macros
disabled. Assumptions!B2 was set to 45 in memory and CalculateFullRebuild ran.
The first chart on Cash 13 weeks stores a fixed layout that Excel's object
model cannot reposition, so a line chart was added in memory to the same
sheet from that chart's two SERIES formulas:
=SERIES('Cash 13 weeks'!$F$1,'Cash 13 weeks'!$B$2:$B$14,'Cash 13 weeks'!$F$2:$F$14,1)
=SERIES('Cash 13 weeks'!$G$1,'Cash 13 weeks'!$B$2:$B$14,'Cash 13 weeks'!$G$2:$G$14,2)
Excel reads every plotted date and value from those workbook cells.

Formatting applied in memory for the website's true-black canvas: chart fill
#000000 with a #262626 border; horizontal gridlines only; the category axis
line drawn at zero; AUD tick labels; fortnightly date labels; the date axis
extended three weeks past 31 December with those extra labels blanked, to
leave room for the end labels; the legend replaced by direct labels for the
lowest week, the final closing cash and the buffer; IBM Plex Sans text from
the same v6.4.2 family the site serves, installed for the exporting user so
the chart matches the page. No value, date or series was altered.

The chart was exported using Excel Chart.Export as PNG at 957 by 479.5
points, yielding 1282 by 639 pixels at this display scale.
The source workbook was closed without saving; its hash remained unchanged.

Weekly closing cash values in the exported chart, AUD:
16800, 15600, 25520, -11960, -11960, -25160, 17640, 27560, 49680,
36480, 79280, 67320, 67320.
The second series is the assumed 15000 cash buffer.
