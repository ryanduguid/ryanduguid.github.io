How the staff cost calculator works

The staff cost calculator adds up the annual cost of one staff member from figures you supply. Annual cost = wages + supplied super amount + other annual costs. Monthly average = annual cost / 12. Include expected insurance, payroll tax, recruitment, relief staff and equipment in other costs. Paid leave already within salary must not be added again.

No statutory rate or threshold is applied.

What the staff cost calculator does not decide

The staff cost calculator does not work out super, payroll tax or award pay. The super amount is the figure you enter; the super guarantee rate is a separate table and is not applied by this calculator.
