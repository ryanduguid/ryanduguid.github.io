How the HELP indexation calculator works

Each 1 June your HELP debt is indexed. The calculator follows the method statement in s 140-5 of the Higher Education Support Act 2003. It takes your accumulated HELP debt from the 1 June a year earlier, adds debts incurred from 1 January to 30 June before that day (each course’s total cut to whole dollars), and subtracts voluntary repayments made from that earlier 1 June to 31 May and compulsory repayments assessed in the same period. It multiplies the result by the indexation factor for the year, then adds debts incurred from 1 July to 31 December, which are not indexed until the next year. Cents are dropped.

From 2023 the factor is the lower of the CPI and WPI factors, which reset the 2023 and 2024 rates to 3.2% and 4%. For 1 June 2025, s 140-26 cuts the amount by 20% before indexation, and s 137-19A cuts a debt incurred from 1 January to 1 June 2025 by 20% too. A voluntary repayment made before 1 June is taken off before indexation; one made on or after 1 June comes off the already indexed debt.

What the HELP indexation calculator does not cover

The calculator does not take a location-preferred HELP debtor’s reduced factor or debt, or VET Student Loan, SFSS or other loans indexed under other Acts. The form takes one debt in each half-year and one repayment; the WebMCP tool takes up to 100 of each, grouped by course, with amended assessments. It does not estimate your balance or work out a compulsory repayment: take both from your ATO account. It does not apply the 20% reduction to any year but 2025. Confirm your balance with the ATO or a registered tax agent.
