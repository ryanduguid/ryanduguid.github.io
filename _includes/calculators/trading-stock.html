Sources applied

  • The change in value: sections 70-35, 70-40, 70-45 and 70-50 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997, on the ATO’s legal database, and the ATO’s Valuing trading stock, read 22 November 2026.
  • The simplified rules: sections 328-285 and 328-295 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997; Schedule 3 item 40(8) of Act No. 92 of 2020 (an entity with aggregated turnover under $50 million, for income years starting on or after 1 July 2021); the ATO’s Simplified trading stock rules, read 22 November 2026.

How the trading stock calculator works

The calculator works one entity’s change in the value of its trading stock for an income year from 2020-21 to 2026-27 under section 70-35. Each item or class on hand at the end of the year is valued at the cost, market selling value or replacement value you elect for it, or at the lowest of the 3; where obsolescence or other special circumstances warrant it, you may elect a lower value under section 70-50, which must be below all 3. The items add up to the stocktake value. If the closing value exceeds the opening value, the excess is assessable income; if it is less, the difference is a deduction. A small business entity, or from 2021-22 an entity with aggregated turnover under $50 million, may choose not to account for the change when the reasonably estimated closing value is within $5,000 of the opening value (section 328-285); the closing value is then the opening value, and nothing is assessable or deductible. Each amount is worked to the cent.

What the trading stock calculator does not cover

The calculator does not work out an item’s cost, its market selling or replacement value, or the reasonable estimate: you enter them as established. It does not decide what is trading stock, and leaves out livestock, the special rules for natural increase, stock taken for private use (section 70-110), disposals outside the ordinary course of business (section 70-90), a change of ownership or residence, and stock in the course of manufacture. It takes up to 4 items or classes on this page and up to 500 through its tool. It refuses the choice where it is not open, and a value elected with no value given. Results are planning estimates: check them against your records and the ATO’s guidance.
