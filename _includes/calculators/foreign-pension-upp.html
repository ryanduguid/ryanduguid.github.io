How the foreign pension undeducted purchase price calculator works

A foreign pension or annuity that you or someone else bought with money that was never deducted has an undeducted purchase price. Section 27H excludes a deductible amount from the pension each year: your share of the annuity, times the purchase price less any residual capital value, divided by the relevant number. The amount excluded cannot be more than the pension, and the rest is assessable income.

For 2025-26 the ATO’s instructions also give 2 shortcuts: 8% of a UK State Pension in category A or a category B widow’s pension, nil for category C or D, and 25% of a Dutch SVB pension whose deductible amount cannot be worked out.

What the foreign pension undeducted purchase price calculator does not cover

The calculator does not work out the purchase price, residual value or life expectation factor, convert foreign currency, or apply a tax treaty. It refuses an annuity partly commuted, one not paid for the whole year, and one whose relevant number the Commissioner must set: for those, ask the ATO for a determination of the deductible amount. It does not cover Australian super income streams. Confirm the figures with the ATO or a registered tax agent.
