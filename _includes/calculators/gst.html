Sources applied

  • Rate: GST is 10% of the GST-exclusive value, so the GST in a GST-inclusive amount is one-eleventh of it. ATO: How GST works, read 20 September 2026; A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999.
  • Applied to your figure as a GST-exclusive amount: GST = amount / 10, rounded once to cents.Applied to your figure as a GST-inclusive amount: GST = amount / 11, rounded once to cents.
  • Supplied by you: the amount, whether it includes GST and that the supply is wholly taxable. Mixed, GST-free and input-taxed supplies are not classified here.

How the GST calculator works

The GST calculator takes one amount for a wholly taxable supply and works out the GST, the GST-exclusive amount and the GST-inclusive amount. For a wholly taxable amount at 10% GST, exclusive amount / 10 gives GST; inclusive amount / 11 gives its GST component. Each result rounds once to cents.

The 10% rate and the one-eleventh fraction come from ATO: How GST works and the A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999.

What the GST calculator does not decide

The GST calculator does not decide whether a supply is taxable, whether you must be registered for GST or what a BAS should report. Mixed, GST-free and input-taxed supplies need classification first.
