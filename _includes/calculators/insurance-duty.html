How the insurance duty calculator works

The calculator works out the duty on one general insurance premium for a contract effected or renewed in 2026-27, at the rate each state’s Act sets for the class you choose. New South Wales charges 9% (Type A) or 5% (Type B), and Type C at 2.5% only for a policy taken out by 31 December 2017; Victoria 10%, or 7% for business insurance; Queensland 9% for class 1 and class 2, and 10 cents on each CTP premium; Western Australia, Tasmania and the Northern Territory 10%; South Australia 11%; and nil in the ACT, whose duty ended on 1 July 2017. The duty is rounded to the cent, half up, with the exact figure kept. It is a port of the insurance-duty command in the australian-accounting repository, checked against that command case by case.

What the insurance duty calculator does not cover

The calculator does not decide which class a policy falls in, what the premium includes, or whether an exemption applies: you state those facts. It does not work life insurance, Queensland accident insurance or CTP insurance outside Queensland, and it refuses a Northern Territory policy for more than one year, whose charge on a whole-term premium the Act leaves unsettled. It does not add GST, the fire services levy or other charges. Results are planning estimates: check them against the insurer’s notice.
