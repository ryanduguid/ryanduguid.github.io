How the super crystallisation calculator works

On 1 July 2007 the old components of a super interest were crystallised into a tax free amount. The law treats a payment of the whole interest as made just before that day. The crystallised segment is the concessional, post-June 1994 invalidity and CGT exempt components, the undeducted contributions and the pre-July 83 component. It stays in the tax free component, and the rest of the value is the taxable component.

The pre-July 83 component is the value less the concessional, invalidity and CGT exempt components, times the days of eligible service before 1 July 1983 over all the days to 30 June 2007. It cannot exceed that base less the undeducted contributions, and it rounds to the cent.

What the super crystallisation calculator does not cover

The calculator takes the non-qualifying and excessive components as nil and does not check the components your fund held. It refuses an interest from an untaxed source and one paying an income stream before 1 July 2007, which have their own rules. It does not work later contributions or the proportioning of a benefit paid now. Confirm the figures with your fund or a registered tax agent.
