Shares or units bought in parcels

This form is for shares or units bought at different times and sold in the 2025-26 or 2026-27 income year. It matches each sale to its parcels, first in, first out or as you select, and works each capital gain or loss and the net capital gain.

Which parcels each sale takes

One parcel a line: a name, the code, the purchase contract date, the units, the purchase cost and the buying costs, then optionally the costs of owning, capital improvements and title costs, separated by spaces, such as A ABC 2020-08-03 1000 5200 19.95. One sale a line: the code, the sale contract date, the units, the proceeds and the selling costs, then, for parcels you select, each as name:units, such as ABC 2026-02-02 1500 13500 29.95.

Sources applied

  • The law: sections 102-5, 104-10, 110-25, 110-55, 112-30, 115-25, 115-100 and 116-40 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 as at 21 May 2026 (Compilation No. 264), the text the CGT calculator above applies. Each parcel is a separate CGT asset, and the costs and proceeds of a sale are shared between its units in proportion.
  • Matching: Taxation Determination TD 33 paragraph 4, which accepts first in, first out where the shares sold cannot be identified, and the taxpayer’s own selection, and ATO: Keeping records of shares and units, both read 27 November 2026.
  • The 12 months: ATO: CGT discount, read 27 November 2026, which leaves out the day bought and the day of the CGT event.
  • The calculation: the cgt-parcels command of australian-accounting, ported line for line and checked against it.
  • Supplied by you: the parcels, the sales, the other gains and losses, and which parcels each sale takes.

What the share parcels form does not cover

The form leaves out parcels bought at or before 21 September 1999. It also leaves out returns of capital, demergers, scrip for scrip and other cost base adjustments, roll-overs, the small business concessions, crypto assets, trading stock and sales from 1 July 2027. Confirm the figure with the ATO or a registered tax agent before relying on it.
