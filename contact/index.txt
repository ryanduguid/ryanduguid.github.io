## Feedback on the cash-flow example

I welcome feedback from accountants and business owners on the fictional Newcastle cash-flow case. Tell me which decision it helps explain, where the working is unclear, or which result you could not reproduce.

For a workbook issue, include your Excel version, receipt-delay setting, the step you took, and what happened. Use the supplied synthetic figures only; do not send client records, credentials, or confidential files. Email ryan@duguid.com.au.

Email links work without scripts. If a link does not open your email app, write to ryan at duguid dot com dot au.

For source code, releases, and issue trackers, use the linked GitHub repositories.

Do not post taxpayer information or client files to public repositories or send them by email.
