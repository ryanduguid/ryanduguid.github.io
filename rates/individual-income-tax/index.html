Rates for 2026-27

15% on taxable income from $18,201 to $45,000. Each rate applies only to the income inside its band, so the base amount is the tax on all the income below the band.

The ATO page also lists resident rates back to 1983-84. They are not listed here.

Medicare levy low-income thresholds

The Medicare levy is 2% of taxable income. A single person not entitled to the seniors and pensioners tax offset pays no levy on taxable income at or below the lower threshold and a reduced levy up to the upper threshold. Thresholds for families and for people entitled to that offset are on the ATO pages and are not listed here.

On 14 November 2026 the ATO’s Medicare levy reduction page gave thresholds for 2025-26 and no later year, so 2026-27 is shown as not published rather than carried forward. The PAYG withholding formulas for payments from 1 July 2026 shade the levy in from $28,011; that is a withholding setting, not a published 2026-27 threshold. The ATO’s myTax instructions for 2019-20 give no threshold table, so that year is not listed.
