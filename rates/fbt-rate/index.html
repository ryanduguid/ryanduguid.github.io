Type 1 applies where the provider is entitled to a GST credit for providing the benefit; Type 2 applies where it is not. Reportable fringe benefits on an employee’s income statement are always grossed up at the Type 2 rate. For the years ending 31 March 2023 to 2027 the reporting threshold is a taxable value above $2,000, a grossed-up minimum of $3,773.

How the rate and the gross-up fit together

FBT is charged on the grossed-up taxable value, not the taxable value itself. The gross-up converts the benefit to the pre-tax salary that would have bought it, so the 47% rate then produces the same tax as the top marginal rate plus Medicare levy. A $10,000 Type 2 benefit grosses up to $18,868 and attracts $8,867.96 of FBT.
