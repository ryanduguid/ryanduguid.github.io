Not yet legislated in the ATO summary

An announcement does not amend the law. Do not automatically apply a proposal in a current-law calculation. Clearly labelled planning scenarios may model proposed changes. The ATO’s guidance on announced measures explains options and consequences for taxpayers who follow existing law or anticipate a change. The Treasury consultation index (archived 6 October 2026) is a historical reference. Check the measure’s current consultation material, parliamentary record and Federal Register entry before assigning a legal status; the archived index does not establish current law. Eight selected rows show TBD and 6 name a bill; the MYEFO philanthropy package shows Various and needs component-level checking.

Negative gearing: application and acquisition cut-off

Schedule 2 item 5 of the Treasury Laws Amendment (Tax Reform No. 1) Act 2026 applies the amendments from the 2027-28 income year. Schedule 2 commenced on 27 June 2026, the day after Royal Assent. The inserted section 26-155(2)(a) excludes amounts relating to an ownership interest in a residential dwelling last acquired before 7:30 pm ACT legal time on 12 May 2026. For this exception, section 26-155(3) uses the contract date for a dwelling acquired under contract, rather than settlement.

Other exceptions cover new residential dwellings and prescribed activities, widely held unit trusts, complying superannuation entities and amounts relating to fringe benefits: sections 26-155(2), (4), (5) and 26-160. New-dwelling and prescribed-activity conditions depend on legislative instruments; this page does not determine whether a particular property qualifies. A 2026-27 purchase can therefore affect later deductions even though the deduction restriction applies from 2027-28.

CGT: separate application rules

The negative gearing acquisition cut-off is not a general CGT grandfathering rule. Schedule 1 has separate rules: items 82 to 84 address income year and CGT-event application, and item 60 inserts transitional section 119-1 for the minimum tax on gains from CGT events on or after 1 July 2027. Check the asset, event and applicable transition provisions separately before calculating a gain.
