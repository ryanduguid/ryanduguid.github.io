## ATO development status unresolved

An announcement does not amend the law. Do not automatically apply a proposal in a current-law calculation. Clearly labelled planning scenarios may model proposed changes. The ATO's guidance on announced measures explains options and consequences for taxpayers who follow existing law or anticipate a change. Check the measure's Treasury consultation, parliamentary record, and Federal Register entry before assigning a legal status. ### Negative gearing: application and acquisition cut-off

Schedule 2 item 5 of the Treasury Laws Amendment (Tax Reform No. 1) Act 2026 applies the amendments from the 2027-28 income year. Schedule 2 commenced on 27 June 2026, the day after Royal Assent. The inserted section 26-155(2)(a) excludes amounts relating to an ownership interest in a residential dwelling last acquired before 7:30 pm ACT legal time on 12 May 2026. For this exception, section 26-155(3) uses the contract date for a dwelling acquired under contract, rather than settlement.

Other exceptions cover new residential dwellings and prescribed activities, widely held unit trusts, complying superannuation entities, and amounts relating to fringe benefits: sections 26-155(2), (4), (5), and 26-160. New-dwelling and prescribed-activity conditions depend on legislative instruments; this page does not determine whether a particular property qualifies. A 2026-27 purchase can therefore affect later deductions even though the deduction restriction applies from 2027-28.

### CGT: separate application rules

The negative-gearing acquisition cut-off is not a general CGT grandfathering rule. Schedule 1 has separate rules: items 82 to 84 address income-year and CGT-event application, and item 60 inserts transitional section 119-1 for the minimum tax on gains from CGT events on or after 1 July 2027. Check the asset, event, and applicable transition provisions separately before calculating a gain.

## Common questions

### Is the $1,000 instant tax deduction law?

Yes. Schedule 4 Part 1 item 17 of the Treasury Laws Amendment (Tax Reform No. 1) Act 2026 applies it from the 2026-27 income year. Section 25-130 covers individuals who are Australian residents at some time in the year and derive assessable labour income. The deduction starts at the lesser of $1,000 and that income, then is reduced, to no less than zero, by specified work-related deductions. It is not an extra $1,000 on top of those same expenses. Income protection, personal sickness and accident insurance premiums, and membership of a trade, business or professional association are excluded from the section 8-1 reduction.

### Is the negative gearing and CGT discount reform law?

Yes. The negative-gearing amendments apply from 2027-28, but an earlier acquisition can affect later treatment. Section 26-155 has a pre-7:30 pm ACT legal time, 12 May 2026 ownership-interest exception and a contract-date rule. CGT has separate application and transition provisions; do not apply that acquisition cut-off to every CGT asset.

### What does TBD mean on the ATO's list?

TBD is the development status reported in the ATO summary. That entry alone does not establish whether a consultation draft, bill, or instrument exists. Check Treasury, Parliament, and the Federal Register for the measure. Various means the package needs component-level checking.

### How often is this page checked?

At the events in the rates register's review cadence: the 1 July rollover, the May Budget, the December MYEFO and each sitting of Parliament, plus any reader report.
