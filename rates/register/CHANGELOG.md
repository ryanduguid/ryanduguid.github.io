# Rates register changelog

One entry per register version. A published row is never edited; a correction
adds a row and marks the old one superseded.

## 2026.09.18

First version. One series.

- `coal-lsl-levy`: added row `2023-07-01`, 2.7 per cent, open ended.
  Source: Coal Mining Industry (Long Service Leave) Payroll Levy Regulations
  2018, s 6, read in Compilation No. 1 (F2023C00557, compilation date 1 July
  2023, registered 7 July 2023, amendments up to F2023L00772) on the Federal
  Register of Legislation, 18 September 2026. Status `verified`, review
  `automated-retrieval`: the compilation was read and the figure matched, and
  no professional review is recorded.

  No row covers eligible wages paid before 1 July 2023. The rate text in force
  before the 2023 amendment was not read, so the register says nothing about
  those months rather than implying the current figure applied then.
