# Rates register changelog

One entry per register version. A published row is never edited; a correction
adds a row and marks the old one superseded.

## 2026.11.05

Check date moved; no figure changed.

- `coal-lsl-levy`, row `2023-07-01`: 2.7 per cent stands. `verified_at` moved
  from 2026-09-18 to 2026-11-05 after re-reading s 6 of the Regulations in
  Compilation No. 1 (F2023C00557) on the Federal Register of Legislation. The
  Register lists no later compilation or amending instrument. Status `verified`,
  review `automated-retrieval`: no professional review is recorded. Coal LSL's
  levy page, read the same day, states a current rate of 2.7%.

  The row now vouches for months ending on or before 5 November 2026, so
  October 2026 is the last whole month covered. November 2026 is not.

## 2026.09.18

First version. One series.

  Source: Coal Mining Industry (Long Service Leave) Payroll Levy Regulations
  2018, s 6, read in Compilation No. 1 (F2023C00557, compilation date 1 July
  2023, registered 7 July 2023, amendments up to F2023L00772) on the Federal
  Register of Legislation, 18 September 2026. Status `verified`, review
