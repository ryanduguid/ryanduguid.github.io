Tax-free threshold claimed (scale 2)

No coefficients below $362 a week. Each range is a weekly earnings amount x less than the figure shown. For example, weekly earnings of $1,000.40 give x = 1,000.99, so y = 0.3227 × 1,000.99 − 185.1935, which rounds to $138.

Scale 2 shades in the Medicare levy from weekly earnings of $538 ($28,011 a year) to $673 ($35,013). Tax offsets claimed on a withholding declaration reduce a scale 2, 5 or 6 weekly amount by 1.9% of the offsets claimed, rounded to the nearest dollar.
