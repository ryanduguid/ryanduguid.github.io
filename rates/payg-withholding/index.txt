# Where does the scale 2 formula start for 2026-27? $362

> $362 a week starts the scale 2 withholding formula for payments from 1 July 2026. The ATO’s Schedule 1 weekly coefficients for scales 1, 2 and 3.

$362

# PAYG withholding: Schedule 1 coefficients

$362 a week. For a payee who claims the tax-free threshold (scale 2), the ATO gives no coefficients for weekly earnings below $362, so nothing is withheld there.

Schedule 1 applies to payments made from 1 July 2026. Withholding is y = ax − b, where x is the whole dollars of weekly earnings plus 99 cents and a and b come from the row whose range holds x. The result is rounded to the nearest dollar, with 50 cents rounding up.

## Tax-free threshold claimed (scale 2)

No coefficients below $362 a week. Each range is a weekly earnings amount x less than the figure shown. For example, weekly earnings of $1,000.40 give x = 1,000.99, so y = 0.3227 × 1,000.99 − 185.1935, which rounds to $138.

## Common questions

### Which scale applies to an employee?

Scale 2 if the payee claimed the tax-free threshold on their TFN declaration, scale 1 if they did not, and scale 3 for a foreign resident. Scales 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 may be used only when the payee has given their TFN.

### How do I work out a fortnightly amount?

Halve the fortnightly earnings, ignore the cents and add 99 cents to get x, work out the rounded weekly amount, then multiply it by 2. The ATO’s Working out the weekly earnings page gives the monthly and quarterly steps.

### What if the payee gave no TFN?

Scale 4 has no coefficients: withhold 47% of the earnings for a resident or 45% for a foreign resident, ignoring cents in the earnings and in the result.

### Do the coefficients include the Medicare levy?

Scales 1, 2, 4 and 6 include it; scale 4 includes it for resident payees only. Scale 3 does not. Scales 5 and 6, for payees claiming a full or half Medicare levy exemption, are on the ATO page.
