# Car limit for depreciation

The car limit is $69,883 for a car first used or leased in 2026-27. It caps the cost you can depreciate for a passenger car designed to carry fewer than nine passengers and a load under one tonne, whatever was paid. It was $69,674 for both 2025-26 and 2024-25.

Each figure below was read on 20 September 2026 from an ATO page: the 2026-27 car thresholds notice, the car limit list for 2021-22 to 2025-26 and the accelerated depreciation page for 2019-20 and 2020-21. The limit itself is section 40-230 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997.

The limit applies in the income year the car is first used or leased. Before applying it, reduce the car's cost by any GST credit you are entitled to claim. GST you cannot claim stays in the cost, including all GST if you are not registered. The ATO no longer publishes an annual determination for the limit; the figure is indexed and announced on its website each June.

## GST credit and luxury car tax

When a car costs more than the limit, the GST credit is generally capped at one-eleventh of the limit: $6,353 for 2026-27, per the ATO's motor vehicle purchase guidance. Luxury car tax paid on the purchase is never creditable. The luxury car tax thresholds are separate figures, $91,661 for fuel-efficient vehicles and $80,809 for other vehicles in 2026-27, and are not maintained here.

## Common questions

### What is the car limit for 2026-27?

$69,883 for a car first used or leased in the 2026-27 income year, per the ATO's car thresholds notice of 9 June 2026.

### Did the car limit change for 2025-26?

No. It stayed at $69,674, the same figure as 2024-25, because the indexation factor for that year did not lift it.

### Which vehicles does the car limit apply to?

Passenger vehicles designed to carry a load of less than one tonne and fewer than nine passengers. A motorcycle or similar vehicle is excluded, as are utes and vans over the one-tonne load rating.

### What is the most GST credit for a car over the limit?

Generally one-eleventh of the car limit: $6,353 for 2026-27. Luxury car tax paid on the car is not creditable.
