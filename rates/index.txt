# Rates

Four maintained Australian reference tables with the current value, source-reviewed date, and primary material kept together.

Verify a figure against current guidance before relying on it. Historical and current values remain on the linked HTML pages. Verified is the date a figure was last checked against its primary source; last reviewed is this page's editorial check.

Last reviewed 20 September 2026.

## Super guarantee rate history

The general super guarantee rate is 12% from 1 July 2025, with no further percentage steps scheduled.

14 September 2026

## Cents per kilometre car-expense rate

The rate is 91 cents for 2026/27 and applies to the first 5,000 business kilometres per car.

Scope

## Announced tax measures not yet law

Fifteen Budget and MYEFO measures have no bill; three are enacted with a 1 July 2027 start. The $1,000 instant deduction and the instant asset write-off are law.
