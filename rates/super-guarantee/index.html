Current rate

12% since 1 July 2025, with no later step scheduled. From 1 July 2026, Payday Super changed the timing: contributions are generally due within 7 business days of each payday rather than quarterly. The rate stayed at 12%, but timing was not the only change: contributions are now worked out on qualifying earnings rather than ordinary time earnings, and the maximum contributions base is an annual limit rather than a quarterly one.

For periods before 2002-03, see former sections 20 and 21 of the Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992.

These are the general rates. Norfolk Island has a separate transitional rate that has been stepping up since 1 July 2016 and reaches 12% on 1 July 2027; the ATO publishes it in a second column of the same table.
