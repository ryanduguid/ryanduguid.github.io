Super guarantee rate history

The general super guarantee rate is 12 per cent. It reached 12 per cent on 1 July 2025 and no further increases are scheduled. Payday super, from 1 July 2026, changed when contributions must be paid, generally within seven business days of each payday, and left the rate alone.

Verified against the ATO key superannuation rates page on 24 August 2026. Sources: ATO, Key superannuation rates and thresholds: Super guarantee, Table 21; Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992.

Current rate

The general super guarantee rate is 12%. It reached 12% on 1 July 2025 and stays at 12% in later years. From 1 July 2026 the timing changes under Payday Super: contributions are generally due within seven business days of each payday rather than quarterly. The rate stays 12%; only the payment schedule changes.

Common questions

What is the super guarantee rate for 2025-26?

12%. The rate reached its final step of 12% on 1 July 2025.

What is the super guarantee rate for 2026-27?

12%. There are no further scheduled increases.

Did the super guarantee rate change on 1 July 2026?

No. Payday Super changed the timing from that date: pay within seven business days of each payday instead of quarterly. The rate itself has been 12% since 1 July 2025.

When did the super guarantee rate stop increasing?

The last increase was to 12% on 1 July 2025. Earlier steps were annual each 1 July: 11.5% (2024), 11% (2023), 10.5% (2022), 10% (2021).
