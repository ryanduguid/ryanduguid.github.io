# Super guarantee rate history

The general super guarantee rate is 12%. It reached 12% on 1 July 2025 and no further increases are scheduled. Payday super, from 1 July 2026, changed when contributions must be paid, generally within 7 business days of each payday, and left the rate alone.

Verified 30 August 2026 against the ATO’s super guarantee rate table, the Treasury Laws Amendment (Payday Superannuation) Act 2025 and the Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992. The general SG rate table was rechecked against the ATO on 14 September 2026.

## Current rate

12% since 1 July 2025, with no later step scheduled. From 1 July 2026, Payday Super changed the timing: contributions are generally due within 7 business days of each payday rather than quarterly. The rate stayed at 12%, but timing was not the only change: contributions are now worked out on qualifying earnings rather than ordinary time earnings, and the maximum contributions base is an annual limit rather than a quarterly one.

These are the general rates. Norfolk Island has a separate transitional rate that has been stepping up since 1 July 2016 and reaches 12% on 1 July 2027; the ATO publishes it in a second column of the same table.

## Common questions

### What is the super guarantee rate for 2025-26?

12%. The rate reached its final step of 12% on 1 July 2025.

### What is the super guarantee rate for 2026-27?

12%. There are no further scheduled increases.

### Did the super guarantee rate change on 1 July 2026?

No. Payday Super changed the timing from that date: pay within 7 business days of each payday instead of quarterly. The rate itself has been 12% since 1 July 2025.

### When did the super guarantee rate stop increasing?

The last increase was to 12% on 1 July 2025. Earlier steps were annual each 1 July: 11.5% (2024), 11% (2023), 10.5% (2022), 10% (2021).
