## GST and BAS

Reviewed 11 September 2026. Use the BAS period, GST accounting basis, and account due date being reviewed.

31. How do I add GST to a price or extract GST from an inclusive amount?

For a wholly taxable sale at 10%, GST is one tenth of the exclusive price or one eleventh of the inclusive price.

- Choose whether your amount already includes GST, then calculate the components.
- Keep the source invoice and confirm that the whole amount is subject to GST.

The calculator does not classify GST-free, input-taxed or mixed supplies.

ATO GST guidance · GST calculator

32. When must my business register for GST?

Check current and projected GST turnover and any compulsory-registration rule for your activity.

- Build a monthly turnover schedule and review what counts towards GST turnover.
- Use the ATO registration guidance to determine the trigger and registration date.

Taxi and ride-sourcing rules and other exceptions mean a general turnover threshold is not enough.

ATO GST guidance

Link to question 32

33. What is the difference between GST-free and input-taxed sales?

GST-free and input-taxed sales both differ from taxable sales, but their credit treatment can differ.

- Classify each income stream using the ATO guidance and retain the basis for the classification.
- Review purchase credits and any apportionment separately for input-taxed activities.

Do not give both categories the same purchase-credit treatment just because no GST appears on the sale.

ATO GST guidance

Link to question 33

34. Do I charge GST when selling to overseas customers?

An overseas customer does not automatically make a sale GST-free.

- Record what you sold, where it is supplied or used, customer circumstances and any export evidence.
- Check the relevant goods or services rule before choosing the GST code.

Export conditions and evidence requirements differ between goods and services.

ATO GST guidance

Link to question 34

35. What information must appear on a valid tax invoice?

Use an invoice checklist matched to the sale and the ATO's tax-invoice requirements.

- Check supplier identity and ABN, date, description, price and how the GST amount is shown.
- Check any buyer-identification requirement and clearly separate taxable and non-taxable items.

Requirements depend on the amount and nature of the sale; a heading alone does not make an invoice valid.

ATO GST guidance

36. How do I prepare a BAS from my accounting records?

Reconcile source records to each BAS label before approval.

- Check reporting period and GST basis, reconcile tax-coded transactions and investigate exceptions.
- Reconcile PAYG and other labels, retain the workpaper and obtain approval before lodgement.

Fictional worked example

For a fictional quarter, reconciled GST on sales at label 1A is $3,000 and eligible GST credits at label 1B are $1,800. The GST component payable is $1,200. Keep the sales and purchase reconciliations with the BAS workpaper, then reconcile other applicable BAS obligations separately. This example assumes the supplied GST amounts are already classified correctly.

The linked readiness gate checks a prepared pack. It does not prepare the BAS, decide tax treatment or authorise lodgement.

37. When is my BAS due, and when must I pay it?

Use the period and due date shown for your BAS account.

- Record both lodgement and payment deadlines and any approved concession.
- Put the cash payment in the forecast and assign someone to retain the receipt.

General quarterly dates can differ from the dates that apply to your account.

ATO GST guidance · 13-week cash forecast

Link to question 37

38. Should I account for GST on a cash or accrual basis?

Compare the timing of receipts and payments under the methods your business is allowed to use.

- Check eligibility and the method currently recorded with the ATO.
- Model the effect on unpaid invoices and bills before changing method, and document transition adjustments.

Changing a report setting in Xero does not by itself change the ATO accounting method.

ATO GST guidance

Link to question 38

39. Why does my BAS GST figure differ from the GST balance in Xero?

Bridge the BAS calculation to the GST control account at the same date and on the same basis.

- Separate opening balances, payments to the ATO, cash-basis timing and journals.
- List each reconciling item and clear unexplained differences before signing off.

A control-account balance is not automatically the GST payable for one BAS period. The linked gate tests supplied tie-outs; it does not investigate reconciling items.

ATO GST guidance · BAS pack readiness checks

Link to question 39

40. How do I correct a mistake in a BAS I have already lodged?

Work out whether the error can be corrected on a later BAS or requires a revision.

- Record the original period, error type, amount, discovery date and supporting transactions.
- Apply the ATO's correction conditions and retain the approved amendment or later-period adjustment.

Do not choose a later-period correction without checking time and value limits.

ATO GST guidance
