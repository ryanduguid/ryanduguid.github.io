## Tax returns

Reviewed 11 September 2026. Use the income year and residency facts for the return being prepared.

1. What will my income-tax bill be?

Start with taxable income for the correct income year, then account for levies, offsets, study loans and tax already withheld. A basic tax-bracket result alone is incomplete.

- Gather income statements, investment statements, deductible expenses and PAYG credits.
- Use the ATO return estimate and reconcile each adjustment before treating it as a budget.

The linked experimental income-tax worksheet excludes levies and offsets; check its supported years.

2. Which expenses reduce my taxable income?

An expense needs a connection to assessable income and the evidence required for that type of claim. Private spending and reimbursed amounts need separate treatment.

- List each expense, its purpose, date, amount and reimbursement.
- Record your work-use calculation and check the ATO category before claiming.

A receipt proves spending, but does not by itself establish deductibility.

3. What steps are involved in completing a return?

Collect the year's records, reconcile them with pre-fill information, calculate supported claims, then review and lodge through an authorised channel.

- Check income statements are ready and account for income missing from pre-fill.
- Keep a copy of the reviewed return, declaration and lodgement receipt.

The checklist prepares your records; lodgement happens with the ATO or your registered agent.

4. Where can I obtain or recover my TFN?

Use the ATO's TFN application or lost-TFN route. The right application process depends on residency and circumstances.

- If you already have a TFN, use the ATO recovery instructions before applying again.
- Complete any identity checks directly with the official service.

Do not put a TFN or identity documents into this website or a public issue.

5. What dates govern the annual tax cycle?

Build a calendar from the dates that apply to your return, activity statements, payroll and entity obligations.

- Record the period, lodgement date, payment date and responsible person for each obligation.
- Confirm the dates in your ATO account or with your registered agent; record any approved extension.

An agent's program or a particular account can have different dates from a general calendar.

6. Which methods can I use to settle my tax bill?

Use the ATO payment instructions for the relevant account and payment reference.

- Check the amount owing and available payment methods in the official service.
- Allow for processing time and keep the receipt; reconcile the payment when it appears.

This website does not collect payments or supply bank details.

7. Which earnings are taxable?

Check each source of income against the ATO's categories. Amounts missing from pre-fill still need review.

- Reconcile employment, business, investment, government and foreign income records.
- List unusual receipts separately and record why they are included or excluded.

A bank deposit is not automatically taxable income, and taxable income is not limited to deposits.

8. How long will the tax-return process take?

Separate your preparation time from the ATO's processing time. Missing records, amendments and reviews can extend the overall process.

- Complete the record checklist before setting a preparation deadline.
- After lodgement, use the official progress service and respond to any information request.

No refund date or processing outcome is guaranteed here.

Individuals can use the lodgement options on the ATO's tax-return page, including myTax where eligible, a registered agent or paper.

- Choose the route for your circumstances and confirm the income year.
- Review the declaration and save the lodgement receipt after submission.

The site supplies preparation guidance and does not submit returns.

10. Which tax rules changed during 2025?

A calendar-year change can affect different income years. For 2025, separate 2024-25 from 2025-26 before comparing rules.

- Use the ATO 'Before you prepare your tax return' guidance for each year.
- Record each changed rate or rule, effective date, source and affected calculation in a change log.

An announcement is not evidence that a measure applied to your transaction. This is a review procedure, not an exhaustive legislative history.

- Keep separately claimed costs out of any category already included in the chosen method.

Keep evidence of hours and costs that supports the particular working-from-home method you use.

## Starting a business

21. Do I need an ABN for freelance or contracting work?

ABN entitlement depends on carrying on or starting an enterprise. An invoice or a client's request alone does not establish it.

- Record the work arrangement, independence, activity and evidence that the enterprise has started.
- Read the ABR entitlement guidance before applying.

An ABN does not decide whether a worker is an employee for tax, super or workplace law.

## GST and BAS

Reviewed 11 September 2026. Use the BAS period, GST accounting basis, and account due date being reviewed.

31. How do I add GST to a price or extract GST from an inclusive amount?

For a wholly taxable sale at 10%, GST is one tenth of the exclusive price or one eleventh of the inclusive price.

- Choose whether your amount already includes GST, then calculate the components.
- Keep the source invoice and confirm that the whole amount is subject to GST.

The calculator does not classify GST-free, input-taxed or mixed supplies.

32. When must my business register for GST?

Check current and projected GST turnover and any compulsory-registration rule for your activity.

- Build a monthly turnover schedule and review what counts towards GST turnover.
- Use the ATO registration guidance to determine the trigger and registration date.

Taxi and ride-sourcing rules and other exceptions mean a general turnover threshold is not enough.

33. What is the difference between GST-free and input-taxed sales?

GST-free and input-taxed sales both differ from taxable sales, but their credit treatment can differ.

- Classify each income stream using the ATO guidance and retain the basis for the classification.
- Review purchase credits and any apportionment separately for input-taxed activities.

Do not give both categories the same purchase-credit treatment just because no GST appears on the sale.

An overseas customer does not automatically make a sale GST-free.

- Record what you sold, where it is supplied or used, customer circumstances and any export evidence.
- Check the relevant goods or services rule before choosing the GST code.

Export conditions and evidence requirements differ between goods and services.

## Cash flow

Reviewed 11 September 2026. Planning method for the dates entered. Include obligations in their expected payment weeks.

61. How do I build a 13-week cash-flow forecast?

Forecast cash by the week it is expected to move through the bank.

- Start with the reconciled bank balance and schedule receipts, suppliers, wages, tax, debt and drawings across 13 weeks.
- Use the browser forecast for a short scenario, or the existing workbook for a detailed worked example.

Fictional worked example

Start a fictional forecast on 28 September 2026 with $200 opening cash and a $100 buffer. Enter $1,000 receipts and $300 payments in week 1, and zero in every later week. Closing cash is $900. Delay the full $1,000 receipt by two weeks: cash closes at minus $100 in weeks 1 and 2, then returns to $900 in week 3. The lowest balance is minus $100, so the funding gap to the buffer is $200. The eventual closing balance is unchanged.

Weekly closing balances can hide a shortage within the week; review daily timing where cash is tight.

## Management reporting

Reviewed 11 September 2026. Compare the same reporting period and basis; historical benchmarks retain their published years.

71. How much must I sell each month to break even?

Break-even sales cover fixed costs after variable costs. For one product, divide fixed costs by contribution per unit.

- Use the same period for fixed costs and the expected selling price and variable cost per unit.
- Round required units up and compare them with practical sales capacity.

The calculator assumes one product or a stable representative mix; it excludes tax and financing cash timing.

72. What is the difference between gross margin and markup?

Gross margin divides gross profit by revenue. Markup divides gross profit by cost.

- Use sales and the directly related cost for the same goods or period.
- Compare both percentages with the calculator and keep GST treatment consistent.

A zero denominator has no meaningful percentage; the calculator leaves that percentage undefined.

73. What hourly rate should I charge to cover costs and earn a target profit?

Divide annual costs plus target profit by realistic billable hours.

- Include the owner's pay target in costs if it must be covered before the stated profit.
- Allow for leave, administration, marketing and unpaid time when estimating billable hours.

The calculated rate is a cost-recovery target before GST; customers' willingness to pay needs separate evidence.

Build contribution by customer, job or service line from consistent revenue and direct-cost assignments.

An allocation method can change apparent profitability without changing total business profit.
