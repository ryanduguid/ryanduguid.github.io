## Management reporting

Reviewed 11 September 2026. Compare the same reporting period and basis; historical benchmarks retain their published years.

71. How much must I sell each month to break even?

Break-even sales cover fixed costs after variable costs. For one product, divide fixed costs by contribution per unit.

- Use the same period for fixed costs and the expected selling price and variable cost per unit.
- Round required units up and compare them with practical sales capacity.

The calculator assumes one product or a stable representative mix; it excludes tax and financing cash timing.

72. What is the difference between gross margin and markup?

Gross margin divides gross profit by revenue. Markup divides gross profit by cost.

- Use sales and the directly related cost for the same goods or period.
- Compare both percentages with the calculator and keep GST treatment consistent.

A zero denominator has no meaningful percentage; the calculator leaves that percentage undefined.

73. What hourly rate should I charge to cover costs and earn a target profit?

Divide annual costs plus target profit by realistic billable hours.

- Include the owner's pay target in costs if it must be covered before the stated profit.
- Allow for leave, administration, marketing and unpaid time when estimating billable hours.

The calculated rate is a cost-recovery target before GST; customers' willingness to pay needs separate evidence.

Choose a pricing strategy · Hourly pricing calculator

74. Which customers, jobs or service lines are making money?

Build contribution by customer, job or service line from consistent revenue and direct-cost assignments.

- Allocate labour using recorded hours and identify material costs, discounts, rework and write-offs.
- Keep shared overhead allocation visible and compare results before and after that allocation.

An allocation method can change apparent profitability without changing total business profit.

Choose a pricing strategy · Margin and markup calculator

75. Why are my actual results different from my budget?

Compare actual and budget figures for the same period and accounting basis, then explain the drivers.

- Calculate the amount and percentage difference for income or costs.
- Split the difference into price, volume, mix, timing, coding and one-off items, then quantify each supported explanation.

The variance calculator measures the difference; it cannot infer its cause from two numbers.

76. Which financial reports should a small-business owner review each month?

Review profit, financial position and cash together, with the balances that explain them.

- Prepare the P&L against budget, balance sheet, cash forecast, aged debtors and creditors, and key obligation reconciliations.
- Record exceptions, decisions, owners and dates for the next review.

Choose detail that changes a decision; a larger dashboard does not necessarily improve the review.

77. How do my business expenses compare with ATO small-business benchmarks?

Choose the ATO industry and turnover range that match your business and reporting period.

- Map ledger accounts to the benchmark definitions and retain the mapping.
- Compare ratios with the relevant dataset and investigate differences in business model, year or coding.

The linked benchmark tool uses specified historical datasets; a difference is not proof of an error or non-compliance.

78. How do I value and report work in progress and unbilled work?

Separate the accounting basis for recognising revenue from the amount billed.

- Reconcile contract value, costs incurred, estimates to complete, billings and unbilled amounts.
- For a suitable construction contract, use the WIP schedule and review estimate changes and profit fade.

The existing tool covers construction cost-to-cost schedules; it does not classify every service contract.

Record keeping for business · Construction WIP schedule

Link to question 78

79. Why has my gross margin fallen even though sales have increased?

Higher sales can coincide with a lower margin when prices, product mix or costs change.

- Compare revenue and direct costs by job or product; check discounts, labour efficiency, rework and cut-off.
- Use the margin and variance calculators, and review cost-to-complete estimates for construction work.

A total-sales comparison alone cannot identify which driver reduced margin.

80. How do I turn Xero data into a useful Excel or Power BI management dashboard?

Start with reconciled exports and a small set of decisions the report needs to support.

- Define account mappings, periods and measures, then tie totals to Xero before adding charts.
- Use the existing trial-balance export and close pack, and document refresh and review steps.

The export tool supplies data; this guide does not install a Power BI connector or grant Xero access.
