## How the Victorian property taxes calculator works

The calculator works out one of 3 Victorian taxes on land for the 2025 or 2026 tax year. Vacant residential land tax is 1% of the capital improved value in the first year the land is liable, 2% in the second and 3% from the third (section 35(3)), and 1% for undeveloped residential land from 2026 or land under the section 88EB concession. The commercial and industrial property tax is nil in both years: land is liable only once its 10-year transition period from its entry date has ended, and no land entered the scheme before 1 July 2024, so the calculator shows when the period ends and the earliest tax year the land can be liable. Windfall gains tax takes the value uplift on each rezoned title, ignores negative uplifts, and charges nothing to $100,000, 62.5% of the part over $100,000 below $500,000, and 50% of the whole uplift from $500,000. It is a port of the vic-property-taxes command in the australian-accounting repository, checked against that command case by case.

## What the Victorian property taxes calculator does not cover

The calculator does not decide whether land is vacant, residential, exempt or rezoned, or what its capital improved value is: you state those facts from the valuation and the land’s use. It does not work ordinary land tax, the vacant residential land tax exemptions, windfall gains tax deferral, exemptions or the Commissioner’s adjustments, or the tax’s due dates. It refuses an entry date on 31 December, where the Acts leave unsettled whether the land is still in its transition period at the moment that fixes liability. Results are planning estimates: check them against the State Revenue Office’s assessment.
