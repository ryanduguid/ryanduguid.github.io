## How the land tax calculator works

The land tax calculator applies one state or territory’s general land tax scale to a total taxable land value and gives the tax for a full year. Below a jurisdiction’s threshold the tax is nil; the Australian Capital Territory has no threshold. Above it, each scale charges a base amount plus a percentage of the value over a band’s threshold, except that Victoria charges a flat $500 from $50,000 and $975 from $100,000 up to $300,000. South Australia charges for every $100, or part of $100, so the excess is rounded up to the next $100. The Australian Capital Territory adds its fixed charge of $1,778 to the valuation charge and assesses each quarter’s share by its days; the calculator gives the full-year amount.

Where a revenue office publishes worked figures, the calculator gives the same amount. Revenue NSW gives $500 on an average land value of $1,100,000. The Victorian State Revenue Office gives $5,550 on $1,100,000. The Queensland Revenue Office gives $1,300 on $680,000 and $87,000 on $6,400,000 for an individual. RevenueSA’s 2026-27 guide gives $2,670 on $1,470,000. For Western Australia, Tasmania and the Australian Capital Territory the tests work examples by hand from the published rates: $6,250 on $1,500,000 in Western Australia, $3,237.50 on $600,000 in Tasmania and $7,418 on an average unimproved value of $600,000 in the Australian Capital Territory.

## What the land tax calculator does not compute

The land tax calculator applies the general scale for an owner taxed at it, such as an individual who is not a foreign person or absentee. It does not decide which land is taxable, which scale applies or what the taxable value is. These are not computed:

- New South Wales: exemptions and concessions, including the principal place of residence and primary production land exemptions, surcharge land tax for foreign owners, and the rates for special and discretionary trusts, which get no threshold.
- Victoria: exemptions and concessions, the trust surcharge rates, the absentee owner surcharge and vacant residential land tax.
- Queensland: exemptions and relief, the rates for companies and trustees, the rates for absentees and the foreign surcharge.
- Western Australia: land tax exemptions and the metropolitan region improvement tax charged with land tax in the Perth metropolitan region.
- South Australia: exemptions, waiver or relief and the trust rates for land held on trust.
- Tasmania: zero rate classifications, exemptions and rebates, including principal residence and primary production land, and the foreign investor land tax surcharge.
- Australian Capital Territory: land tax exemptions, the foreign ownership surcharge, a unit’s share by its unit entitlement and the split into quarters. Land tax there applies to residential property that is not a principal place of residence.

Joint ownership, grouping of related companies and the apportionment of the tax between properties are not computed either. Confirm the figure with the revenue office’s own calculator or assessment notice before relying on it.
