A line marked for the remission states that the manufacturer meets item 10 (a), (b), (d) and (e) of the Excise Regulation 2015: duty payable by it, independence, at least 70% fermented or distilled by it and, for a distillery after its first 2 years, the still test. The cap is $350,000 a financial year to 2025-26 and $400,000 from 2026-27.

## How the alcohol excise calculator works

The calculator works the excise duty on beer, spirits, brandy and other excisable beverages entered for home consumption in a financial year from 2022-23 to 2026-27, up to 31 January 2027. Each line is classified to its Schedule subitem by product, strength and, for beer, the container: over 48 litres, or 8 to 48 litres designed for a pressurised gas or pump system, takes the draught rate. Litres of alcohol are the volume times the strength, and for beer only the strength above 1.15%. Where the label states a higher strength, duty is on the labelled one (section 77FA). Each line takes the rate in force on the day it is entered. Goods of one subitem at one rate are one return line: their litres of alcohol are truncated to 0.1 litre and the duty to the cent, as the ATO’s guidelines allow. The alcohol manufacturer remission is applied to the lines that claim it, up to the cap left for the year: $350,000 to 2025-26 and $400,000 from 2026-27, reduced by days for a manufacturer that started during the year. Where the duty was already paid, the remission is a refund on application.

## What the alcohol excise calculator does not cover

The calculator does not cover wine, which bears wine equalisation tax, imported goods, which bear customs duty, tobacco, fuel, concessional spirit under subitems 3.5 to 3.8, or liquor made for non-commercial purposes. It does not decide whether a manufacturer meets the remission’s independence, 70% and still tests, or whether a business holds a licence or periodic settlement permission. It holds no rate for goods entered after 31 January 2027, before the next indexation was published, and refuses a labelled strength that would move the goods to another subitem. It takes up to 3 lines on this page and up to 1,000 through its tool. Results are planning estimates: check them against your excise returns and the ATO’s guidance.
