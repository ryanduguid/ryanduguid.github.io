## How the portable LSL levy calculator works

Each state and territory runs portable long service leave schemes for some industries, funded by a levy. The calculator ports the state and territory part of the portable-lsl-levy command of australian-accounting. The construction levies in New South Wales, Queensland and the Northern Territory are on the project’s cost of work: 0.25% from $250,000 in NSW, rounded down to the dollar; 0.575% from $150,000 in Queensland, in 3 parts (0.35% portable leave, 0.1% CSQ training and 0.125% WHSQ), each rounded to the nearest dollar with 50 cents down; and 0.1% from $1 million to $5 billion in the NT. Every other scheme is a rate on ordinary wages, rounded half up to the cent: Victoria, South Australia, Western Australia, the ACT and Tasmania for construction, and the community services, contract cleaning and security schemes where they run. Each scheme has the years whose rate was read.

## What the portable LSL levy calculator does not cover

The calculator does not decide which workers or work a scheme covers, what counts as ordinary pay or as the cost of work, or apprentice and trainee exemptions: you state the figures. It refuses partial or special project exemptions, NT projects over $5 billion and the years a scheme’s rate was not read for. It leaves out the ACT services discounts for food and beverage businesses from 1 January 2027, late payment interest, penalties, instalments, refunds and lodgment. Coal LSL has its own Coal LSL levy calculator. Check the levy against the board’s return portal before paying.
