## How the non-resident withholding calculator works

A payer of a dividend, interest or a royalty to a foreign resident withholds an amount under Subdivision 12-F of Schedule 1 to the Taxation Administration Act 1953 and pays it to the ATO. The calculator ports the non-resident-withholding command of australian-accounting for those 3 payments. The base is the payment, less a dividend’s franked part and conduit foreign income. Its cents are dropped and the regulation rate applied: 30% for a dividend or royalty, or the treaty rate you enter, and 10% for interest, again dropping cents (regulations 26 and 40 to 42). Section 12-300 caps the amount at the withholding tax payable less anything already withheld.

## What the non-resident withholding calculator does not cover

The calculator does not decide whether the payee is a foreign resident, which treaty applies or its rate, whether an exemption such as section 128F applies, or whether a payment is a dividend, interest or a royalty: you enter those as given. It does not cover managed investment trust fund payments (Subdivision 12-H), casino gaming junkets, entertainment or sports payments, construction payments, foreign resident capital gains withholding, non-cash benefits or the annual report. Have a registered tax agent confirm the figures and the result.
