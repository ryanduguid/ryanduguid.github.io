GST

For a wholly taxable amount at 10% GST, exclusive amount / 10 gives GST; inclusive amount / 11 gives its GST component. Each result rounds once to cents. Mixed, GST-free and input-taxed supplies need classification first.

Business-use share

Eligible cost × supported business-use percentage / 100. This apportions the amount you supply. It does not determine deductibility, depreciation or record sufficiency.

Margin and markup

Gross profit = sales − direct cost. Margin = gross profit / sales × 100. Markup = gross profit / direct cost × 100. Use a consistent GST basis and matching period. A zero denominator leaves that percentage undefined.

Break-even sales

Contribution per unit = price − variable cost per unit. Required units = fixed costs / contribution, rounded up. Required sales = whole units × price. Use one product or a stable representative mix and a consistent GST basis.

Hourly pricing

Required hourly rate = (annual costs + target annual profit) / annual billable hours. Include owner remuneration in costs if it must be covered before profit. Exclude non-billable time from hours. The result is before GST.

Budget variance

Difference = actual − budget. Percentage = difference / absolute budget × 100. A zero budget has no percentage comparison. Positive income differences are favourable; positive cost differences are unfavourable. Explain causes from the records.

Monthly loan payments

For fixed monthly interest r and n payments: payment = principal × r / (1 − (1 + r) raised to −n). At zero interest, payment = principal / n. Payments are in arrears, with no fees or balloon. Interest is estimated from unrounded payments; lender rounding and daily accrual can differ.

Staff cost

Annual cost = wages + supplied super amount + other annual costs. Monthly average = annual cost / 12. Include expected insurance, payroll tax, recruitment, relief staff and equipment in other costs. Paid leave already within salary must not be added again.

13-week cash forecast

Weekly closing cash = opening cash + receipts − payments. Each closing balance opens the next week. Funding gap = minimum buffer − lowest opening or weekly closing cash, with a floor of zero. It is a planning gap, not a lending decision. Weekly totals can hide a shortage within the week.
