## How the depreciation pools calculator works

The calculator works one of 3 Division 40, Subdivision 328-D or Division 43 worksheets for an income year from 2020-21 to 2026-27. A low-value pool declines by 18.75% of the low-cost assets and second element costs added in the year, and by 37.5% of the previous closing pool balance and the low-value assets added (section 40-440). The 2 parts are added before rounding. Termination values reduce the closing balance, and any excess over it is assessable income (section 40-445).

A general small business pool first adds the opening balance, the year’s additions and cost additions, less termination values (subsection 328-210(2)). An amount above zero and below the year’s low pool value threshold is deducted in full and the pool closes at zero: $20,000 from 2023-24, and no limit from 2020-21 to 2022-23. An amount below zero is assessable income (section 328-215). Otherwise the deduction is 30% of the opening balance and 15% of the additions and cost additions (section 328-190), each rounded to the cent. The ATO’s example of Loretta’s pool, with a $100,000 opening balance, $28,000 of additions and an $8,000 sale, gives a $34,200 deduction and an $85,800 closing balance.

A capital works deduction is your construction expenditure times the days used over 365, at 4% for a part used in the 4% manner or else 2.5%, then reduced to the taxable use and limited to the undeducted construction expenditure (section 43-210, or section 43-215 for works begun from 16 September 1987 to 26 February 1992). The ATO’s example 29 in Rental properties 2026, $115,800 for 346 days at 2.5%, gives $2,744.

## What the depreciation pools calculator does not cover

The calculator does not decide which assets may go into a pool, whether you are a small business entity that chose simplified depreciation, or each asset’s taxable use: you enter the amounts after those choices. It does not work out temporary full expensing of individual assets from 2020-21 to 2022-23, the instant asset write-off or backing business investment, and refuses the one 2020-21 small business pool case where the 57.5% rate could apply. It leaves out the change of business use adjustment, roll-over relief, the car limit and balancing adjustments outside a pool. For capital works it refuses works begun before 16 September 1987 and the 4% rate for works begun before 27 February 1992, and does not decide whether the works qualify, work out the undeducted construction expenditure or split it amongst co-owners. Results are planning estimates: check them against your records and the ATO’s guidance.
