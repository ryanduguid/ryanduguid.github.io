## How the margin and markup calculator works

The margin and markup calculator works out gross profit, margin and markup from one sales figure and one direct cost figure. Gross profit = sales − direct cost. Margin = gross profit / sales × 100. Markup = gross profit / direct cost × 100. A zero denominator leaves that percentage undefined.

No statutory rate or threshold is applied. The definitions follow business.gov.au: pricing.

## What the margin and markup calculator does not decide

The margin and markup calculator does not set a price, test whether direct cost is complete or adjust for GST. Use a consistent GST basis and matching period for both figures.
