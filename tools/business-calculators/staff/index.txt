Calculate Enter the inputs and calculate.

## How it works

Annual cost = wages + supplied super amount + other annual costs. Monthly average = annual cost / 12. Include expected insurance, payroll tax, recruitment, relief staff and equipment in other costs. Paid leave already within salary must not be added again.
