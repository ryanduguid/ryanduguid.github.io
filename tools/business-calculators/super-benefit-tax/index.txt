## What the inputs need

The most tax is what the lump sum can bear in the person’s return, before the Medicare levy. The amount withheld is what the fund takes out when it pays, under Schedule 12 of the withholding schedules; it is a credit against that tax. A departing Australia superannuation payment, to a former temporary resident who has left Australia, bears its own final tax.

For one lump sum from a complying fund paid on a condition of release: not an income stream commutation or a payment in breach of the payment rules. A roll-over and a payment to the trustee of a deceased estate are worked only for withholding. The fund states the interest’s value and tax free component just before the payment, and the untaxed element.

The lump sum’s tax free component is its share of the interest’s, rounded to the cent; the rest is taxable, taxed in the fund except the untaxed element. Leave that at 0 for a fund that pays tax on its income, as most do.

Needed only with an untaxed element. An untaxed element from service before 1 July 1983 is refused: part of it then becomes tax free under section 307-150.

You decide whether the person is a death benefits dependant: a spouse or former spouse, a child under 18, someone interdependent with or dependent on the member, or anyone if the member died in the line of duty (section 302-195).

Two registered medical practitioners, one a specialist, certify death is likely within 24 months. A disability superannuation benefit is refused: section 307-145 enlarges its tax free component.

The low rate cap applies only at preservation age and under 60, which from 1 July 2024 no one is. Enter the amounts counted for earlier lump sums. Other lump sums at that age in the same year share the cap, so they are refused while any is left.

### What the withholding and DASP inputs need

Withholding is worked for payments from 13 October 2020. With a TFN, a resident’s rate for each part is its ceiling rate plus the 2% Medicare levy, and the total rounds to the nearest dollar. Without one, the fund withholds 47% (45% for a foreign resident) of the taxable component, or of the untaxed element at 60 or over, ignoring cents. Without a TFN the rate turns on the payee’s age, which the calculator does not take for a death benefit, and none is stated for a member benefit under $200, so a non-dependant’s death benefit and such a member benefit are refused. The calculator assumes the payment is taxable in Australia and applies no tax treaty.

Take the components from the fund’s or the ATO’s statement. The working holiday maker rate applies when the payment includes amounts from contributions made whilst the person was a working holiday maker. An unclaimed money roll-over with an untaxed element is refused: section 5(2) taxes any excess untaxed roll-over amount in it at nil.

### What the income stream inputs need

An income stream payment is worked under Schedule 13, Part A, for payments in 2024-25 to 2026-27. Its tax free component is its share of the interest’s when the stream commenced. The fund withholds from the taxable component under 60, or only from the untaxed element at 60 or over, ignoring cents, by the PAYG tax table for the period: scale 2 with the tax-free threshold claimed, scale 1 without. It takes off a tax offset (15% of the taxed element for a disability super income stream or a dependant under 60, 10% of the untaxed element at 60 or over) and withholds the greater of the rest and the offset adjustment for the Medicare levy. Without a TFN it withholds 47% (45% for a foreign resident) with no offset. A capped defined benefit income stream to someone 60 or over is refused, as Parts B to E apply, as are a foreign resident with a TFN and a dependant under 60 whose deceased died at 60 or over.

## How the super lump sum tax calculator works

The super lump sum tax calculator splits one lump sum from a complying fund, paid from 1 July 2020 to 30 June 2027, into its components. It gives the most income tax each part can bear before the Medicare levy. A member’s treatment turns on age on the payment day and preservation age; a death benefit’s turns on whether the person receiving it is a death benefits dependant. Each rate is a ceiling: the offsets stop the tax on a part going above it, so a person on lower marginal rates pays less, and the Medicare levy is added on top.

It also works the amount the fund withholds from the same lump sum under Schedule 12, for a payment from 13 October 2020, including from a roll-over and a death benefit paid to a deceased estate; the amount withheld from one income stream payment under Schedule 13, Part A, from 1 July 2024; and the final tax on a departing Australia superannuation payment in 2025-26 to 2027-28.

Its results match the super-benefit-tax command of Ryan Duguid’s australian-accounting calculators on the ATO’s examples and 532 test cases, and its super-withholding and dasp-tax commands on 613 more and the super-withholding command’s income stream withholding on 359 more, refusals included.

## What the super lump sum tax calculator does not cover

The calculator does not work the tax at the person’s own marginal rates, the Medicare levy or the tax on an income stream in the person’s return. Nor does it work withholding from a capped defined benefit income stream to someone 60 or over (Schedule 13 Parts B to E), a commutation of an income stream, the tax on a roll-over or a deceased estate, a tax treaty, or payments from a non-complying or foreign fund. It does not decide dependency, a terminal medical condition, the components or the elements: you state those. It refuses a disability superannuation benefit, an untaxed element from service before 1 July 1983, and other lump sums in the same year that share the low rate cap.

Confirm the result with the fund, the ATO’s guidance on super lump sums or a registered tax agent before relying on it. The redundancy and ETP withholding calculator covers employment termination payments.
