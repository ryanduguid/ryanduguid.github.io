## How the break-even calculator works

The break-even sales calculator works out how many whole units, and how much in sales, cover the fixed costs for a period. Contribution per unit = price − variable cost per unit. Required units = fixed costs / contribution, rounded up. Required sales = whole units × price. Price must exceed variable cost per unit, or no break-even exists.

No statutory rate or threshold is applied. The definitions follow Business Queensland: break-even and profit.

## What the break-even calculator does not decide

The break-even sales calculator does not forecast demand or say that the units can be sold. It uses one price and one variable cost, so use one product or a stable representative mix and a consistent GST basis.
