## Monthly loan payments

For fixed monthly interest r and n payments: payment = principal × r / (1 − (1 + r) raised to −n). At zero interest, payment = principal / n. Payments are in arrears, with no fees or balloon. Interest is estimated from unrounded payments; lender rounding and daily accrual can differ.
