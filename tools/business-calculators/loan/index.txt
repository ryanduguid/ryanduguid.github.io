## How the monthly loan payment calculator works

The monthly loan payment calculator works out a fixed monthly payment, the interest and principal in the first payment, and estimated total interest. For fixed monthly interest r and n payments: payment = principal × r / (1 − (1 + r) raised to −n). At zero interest, payment = principal / n.

A balloon, or residual, is a lump sum still owed at the end of the term, as in vehicle and equipment finance. Its value today, balloon × (1 + r) raised to −n, comes off the principal before the payment is worked out. Payments in advance fall at the start of each month, so the first carries no interest and each payment is the arrears payment divided by (1 + r). This is the same payment as a spreadsheet’s PMT with the balloon as the future value and type 1 for advance. Total interest is the payments plus the balloon, less the principal.

No statutory rate or external source is applied. The interest rate is yours, not a market or lender rate.

## What the monthly loan payment calculator leaves out

The monthly loan payment calculator assumes a rate that does not change and no fees. It does not work out GST, stamp duty or the tax treatment of the interest or the vehicle. Total interest uses the unrounded payment and rounds once, so it is an estimate; lender rounding and daily accrual can differ. For $100,000 at 6% over 360 months it gives $115,838.19, where a cent-by-cent schedule of $599.55 payments, with interest rounded each month and a final payment of $600.00, gives $115,838.45. The result is not a lender’s quote.
