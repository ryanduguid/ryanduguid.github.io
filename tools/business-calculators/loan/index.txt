## How it works

For fixed monthly interest r and n payments: payment = principal × r / (1 − (1 + r) raised to −n). At zero interest, payment = principal / n. Payments are in arrears, with no fees or balloon. Total interest uses the unrounded payment and rounds once, so it is an estimate; lender rounding and daily accrual can differ. For $100,000 at 6% over 360 months it gives $115,838.19, where a cent-by-cent schedule of $599.55 payments, with interest rounded each month and a final payment of $600.00, gives $115,838.45.
