## What each line needs

Each line is one sale, importation, adjustment or refund in the tax period. Fill the lines in order.

For a business registered or required to be registered for GST. You establish each line: that the vehicle is a car and not one the Act excludes (an emergency vehicle, a car fitted out for wheelchairs, a commercial vehicle not designed mainly to carry passengers, a motor home or campervan), that the supply or importation is taxable (no quote, not GST-free or input taxed), its luxury car tax value and its combined fuel consumption rating.

The value is the luxury car tax value, with GST and customs duty but not luxury car tax; for a price adjustment, the corrected value. Leave the litres blank for a car with no combined fuel consumption rating.

An adjustment or refund takes the year the car was supplied or imported in, which can be before the tax period’s; a refund for a car supplied or imported before 1 July 2019 is refused.

Needed only for a car rated above 3.5 and up to 7 litres, supplied or imported from 1 July 2025. Answer yes if, before that day, it was supplied or imported and used in Australia other than for a quotable purpose: the 7 litre rating then continues.

The ATO describes a refund-eligible car as a 4 wheel or all wheel drive that is either a passenger car (category MA) with a ground clearance of at least 175 mm or an off-road passenger vehicle (category MC). The claimant must be registered for GST (sections 18-5 and 18-10).

## How the luxury car tax calculator works

The luxury car tax calculator works out the tax on a tax period’s sales and importations of luxury cars in 2019-20 to 2026-27, with the price and change of use adjustments and labels 1E and 1F on the business activity statement. Each car takes the threshold for the financial year of its supply or importation, or the fuel-efficient car limit for a car rated at most 3.5 litres per 100 km from 1 July 2025 and at most 7 litres before. The tax is 33% of the GST-exclusive value over that threshold. Tax on an importation is shown apart, because it is paid to the Department of Home Affairs with any customs duty. A primary producer’s or tourism operator’s refund of the tax borne on a refund-eligible car, up to $10,000, is shown apart too, because it is claimed on the ATO’s form.

Its results match the luxury-car-tax command of Ryan Duguid’s australian-accounting calculators on the ATO’s examples and 309 test cases, refusals included.

## What the luxury car tax calculator does not cover

The calculator does not decide whether a vehicle is a car or a luxury car, whether you could quote or a supply is taxable, the value of a car sold with other goods, or whether a car is refund-eligible: you state those. It does not work bad debt adjustments, the adjustments in subsections 15-30(1A) and (3A), a public institution’s adjustment, the credit for an entity that is not registered, the refund for cars supplied before 1 July 2019, or which tax period a line belongs to. It refuses a tax period outside 2019-20 to 2026-27, a supply year outside 2012-13 to the tax period’s, and a second primary producer refund in the period. A primary producer’s refund in an earlier period of the same year also rules out another, which the page cannot see.

Confirm the result with the ATO’s luxury car tax guidance or a registered tax or BAS agent before relying on it. The vehicle classifier shows whether a vehicle is a car for luxury car tax.
