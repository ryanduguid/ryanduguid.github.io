# Business-use share calculator

Apportion an eligible cost by a supported business-use percentage. It does not decide deductibility, depreciation or record sufficiency.

One of the nine business calculators. Calculations run in this browser; inputs are not saved or sent anywhere.

Planning estimates in AUD. Confirm tax treatment, liability amounts and dates separately. Amounts round to the nearest cent, with exact half cents rounded up.

Published 20 September 2026. Last reviewed 20 September 2026.

JavaScript is required for interactive calculations. The formula, assumptions and source links remain available below.

## Business-use share

Eligible cost × supported business-use percentage / 100. This apportions the amount you supply. It does not determine deductibility, depreciation or record sufficiency.

For car expenses under the cents per kilometre method, the rate by income year is kept as a maintained table on this site.
