# Business calculators

Twenty calculators, each on its own page with the formula, assumptions and source beside the inputs. The calculator code runs in your browser. It does not send your inputs to a server or save them automatically.

Find a question and prepare a checklist.

Planning estimates in AUD. Confirm tax treatment, liability amounts and dates separately. Amounts round to the nearest cent, with exact half cents rounded up, and percentages to two decimal places, with exact halves rounded away from zero; loan estimates allow for calculation precision.

Published 11 September 2026. Last checked 17 November 2026, the newest of the calculators’ source checks.

## Pricing, planning and loans

- GST calculator Work out GST, the GST-exclusive amount and the GST-inclusive amount for a wholly taxable supply at 10%, rounded once to cents.
- Business use share calculator Apportion an eligible cost by a supported business use percentage. It does not decide deductibility, depreciation or record sufficiency.
- Margin and markup calculator Gross profit, margin and markup from sales and direct cost, on a consistent GST basis and matching period.
- Break-even sales calculator Whole units and sales required to cover fixed costs from price and variable cost per unit.
- Hourly pricing calculator Required hourly rate before GST from annual costs, target profit and billable hours.
- Fixed fee pricing calculator A fixed fee from estimated hours at a rate or a cost and margin, with GST, monthly instalments and the effective hourly rate.
- Budget variance calculator Actual minus budget, the percentage of absolute budget and whether the difference is favourable or unfavourable.
- Monthly loan payment calculator Fixed monthly payment, first-payment interest and principal, and estimated total interest, in arrears or in advance, with an optional balloon.

## Division 7A, FBT, professional firms and GIC

- Division 7A loan calculator Minimum yearly repayment, interest and principal on one amalgamated loan.
- FBT car calculator Taxable value and FBT on one car for the FBT year ending 31 March 2027, by the statutory formula or operating cost method.
- Vehicle classifier Whether a vehicle is a car, and what follows for the car limit, the GST credit, luxury car tax, FBT and records.
- PCG 2021/4 risk scorer The ATO’s risk zone for a professional firm profit allocation, after both gateways.
- GIC payment plan calculator What a tax debt costs by equal instalments, against paying it now, with GIC compounding daily.
- Division 296 tax calculator One person’s Division 296 tax on superannuation earnings for 2026-27, above $3 million and above $10 million.

## Staff and cash

- Staff cost calculator Annual and monthly cost of a staff member from wages, the supplied super amount and other annual costs.
- 13-week cash forecast Weekly closing cash from opening cash, receipts and payments, with one delayed receipt and a funding gap against a buffer. Save, load and export as CSV.

## Objections, instalments, termination, land tax and duty

- Objection deadline checker The last day to object to an income tax or FBT assessment, an amended assessment or another ATO decision, with the weekend rule.
- PAYG instalments calculator The quarterly PAYG instalment from the notified amount, a varied amount, instalment income times the rate, or a varied rate with its credit.
- Redundancy and ETP withholding The tax-free part of a genuine redundancy payment and the amount to withhold from an ETP under Schedule 11, for 2026-27.
- Land tax calculator Land tax for a full year in each of the 7 states and territories that levy it, at the general or trust rates, with a foreign or absentee owner surcharge and WA’s metropolitan region improvement tax where chosen, before any exemption.
- Transfer duty calculator General transfer (stamp) duty on a residential purchase in each of the 8 states and territories, and the duty for an eligible first home buyer of a home.
