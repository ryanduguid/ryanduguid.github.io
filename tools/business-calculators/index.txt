## Pricing, planning and loans

- GST calculator Work out GST, the GST-exclusive amount and the GST-inclusive amount for a wholly taxable supply at 10%, rounded once to cents.
- GST margin scheme calculator Whether the margin scheme can apply to a sale of land, a stratum unit or a long-term lease, the margin and its GST, and the GST it saves.
- GST adjustments calculator The 6 GST adjustments and tests.
- Business use share calculator Apportion an eligible cost by a supported business use percentage. It does not decide deductibility, depreciation or record sufficiency.
- Margin and markup calculator Gross profit, margin and markup from sales and direct cost, on a consistent GST basis and matching period.
- Break-even sales calculator Whole units and sales required to cover fixed costs from price and variable cost per unit.
- Hourly pricing calculator Required hourly rate before GST from annual costs, target profit and billable hours.
- Fixed fee pricing calculator A fixed fee from estimated hours at a rate or a cost and margin, with GST, monthly instalments and the effective hourly rate.
- Budget variance calculator Actual minus budget, the percentage of absolute budget and whether the difference is favourable or unfavourable.
- Loan repayment schedule calculator Every payment of a loan, weekly to yearly, in arrears or in advance, with an optional balloon; works out the payment, the amount, the term or the rate, with CSV export.
- Hire purchase and chattel mortgage schedule The same schedule for a hire purchase agreement or chattel mortgage, with the interest by income year and a journal for each payment.
- AASB 16 lease calculator A lessee’s lease liability, right-of-use asset, interest and depreciation by income year, with the journals, beside hire purchase and buying outright after GST and tax.
- Borrowing expenses calculator Section 25-25 borrowing expenses by income year from 2017-18, spread by days, with the $100 rule, refinancing and an optional AASB 9 column.
- Depreciation pools and capital works calculator A low-value pool at 18.75% and 37.5%, a general small business pool at 15% and 30% with the low pool value write-off, or a capital works deduction at 2.5% or 4%, for 2020-21 to 2026-27.
- Rental property calculator One owner’s rental income and deductions for a year: expenses by days let, interest on a mixed-use loan, capital works and second-hand assets, the net rent and its effect on tax.

## Division 7A, FBT, professional firms and GIC

- Division 7A loan calculator Minimum yearly repayment, interest and principal on one amalgamated loan.
- FBT car calculator Taxable value and FBT on one car for the FBT year ending 31 March 2027, by the statutory formula or operating cost method.
- FBT car parking calculator Whether car parking fringe benefits arise against the $11.48 threshold, and their taxable value by any of the 5 methods, for the FBT year ending 31 March 2027.
- FBT expense payment calculator Taxable value of an expense payment fringe benefit, external with the otherwise deductible rule or in-house by the in-house rules, for the FBT year ending 31 March 2027.
- FBT loan calculator Taxable value of a loan fringe benefit at the 8.27% statutory interest rate, with the otherwise deductible rule, for the FBT year ending 31 March 2027.
- Meal entertainment calculator Taxable value of meal entertainment fringe benefits by the 50/50 split or 12-week register method, for the FBT year ending 31 March 2027.
- FBT property calculator Taxable value of a property fringe benefit by the in-house or external rules, with the otherwise deductible rule and the $1,000 in-house reduction, for the FBT year ending 31 March 2027.
- Vehicle classifier Whether a vehicle is a car, and what follows for the car limit, the GST credit, luxury car tax, FBT and records.
- PCG 2021/4 risk scorer The ATO’s risk zone for a professional firm profit allocation, after both gateways.
- PCG 2021/4 allocation Each recipient’s tax on a professional firm’s profit, the effective tax rate and the ATO’s risk zone.
- GIC payment plan calculator What a tax debt costs by equal instalments, against paying it now, with GIC compounding daily.
- ATO penalties and interest calculator GIC and SIC by day and quarter, the failure to lodge penalty, statement penalties with increases and reductions, and the interest the ATO pays, all before remission.
- Division 7A loan schedule calculator A complying Division 7A loan year by year over 7 or 25 years: each minimum repayment, the interest, extra repayments and any shortfall taken to be a dividend.
- Division 296 tax calculator One person’s Division 296 tax on superannuation earnings for 2026-27, above $3 million and above $10 million.
- PSI rules checker Whether the personal services income rules apply, step by step through Divisions 84 to 87.

- Franking account calculator A company’s franking account for 2025-26 or 2026-27: franking deficit tax and its offset, the benchmark rule and the franking return’s labels and due date.
- Company tax and franking calculator A company’s 25% or 30% rate from the base rate entity test, its rate for franking dividends, the maximum franking credit and a shareholder’s tax or refund.
- R&D tax offset calculator A company’s R&D tax incentive offset for 2021-22 to 2026-27, refundable or with the premium tiers, and the amount a grant or feedstock clawback includes in assessable income.
- Deferred tax calculator A company’s AASB 112 tax effect worksheet: deferred tax liability and asset, the movement, current tax and the reconciliation.
- Salary against dividends calculator A company owner’s salary and super guarantee against fully franked dividends for 2021-22 to 2026-27: the cash and super kept after tax, and the split that keeps the most.

## Staff, payroll and termination

- Staff cost calculator Annual and monthly cost of a staff member from wages, the supplied super amount and other annual costs.
- 13-week cash forecast Weekly closing cash from opening cash, receipts and payments, with one delayed receipt and a funding gap against a buffer. Save, load and export as CSV.
- SGC and Payday Super calculator When a Payday Super contribution is due, how many days late it was, and one employee’s super guarantee charge, each step cited.
- Long service leave calculator One employee’s long service leave under each state and territory’s Act: the leave to take now, or the weeks payable when employment ends for a given reason and their value, each step cited.
- Workers compensation premium calculator A 2026-27 premium from the declared wages and the insurer’s rate under each state and territory’s scheme, with GST, duty and the actual wages adjustment, each step cited.
- Super lump sum tax The components of a super lump sum and the most tax each part can bear, for members and death benefits, 2020-21 to 2026-27.
- Redundancy and ETP withholding The tax-free part of a genuine redundancy payment and the amount to withhold from an ETP under Schedule 11, for 2026-27.
- Unused leave on termination Schedule 7 withholding and the lump sum A, B and type U amounts for unused leave paid on termination, for 2026-27.

## State taxes, property and first home buyers

- Transfer duty calculator General transfer (stamp) duty on a residential purchase in each of the 8 states and territories, and the duty for an eligible first home buyer of a home.
- First home owner grant calculator The grant in each of the 8 states and territories for a new home, by contract date and value cap, beside the first home buyer transfer duty.
- Land tax calculator Land tax for a full year in each of the 7 states and territories that levy it, at the general or trust rates, with a foreign or absentee owner surcharge and WA’s metropolitan region improvement tax where chosen, before any exemption.
- Foreign purchaser surcharge calculator Foreign purchaser duty and the foreign owner land tax surcharge in each state and territory, with each one’s own test of who is foreign, the 2026-27 FIRB fee and the upfront total in all 8 side by side.
- Payroll tax calculator One employer’s annual payroll tax for 2026-27 in any state or territory, for an employer outside a group, with each step cited.

- PAYG instalments calculator The quarterly PAYG instalment from the notified amount, a varied amount, instalment income times the rate, or a varied rate with its credit.
- Objection deadline checker The last day to object to an income tax or FBT assessment, an amended assessment or another ATO decision, with the weekend rule.
- Fuel tax credit calculator A business’s fuel tax credits at the ATO’s rate for each day since 1 July 2022, by fuel and use, with label 7D and the small claimant methods.
- Luxury car tax calculator Luxury car tax, its adjustments and refunds for 2019-20 to 2026-27.
- Wine equalisation tax calculator WET on a tax period’s wholesale sales, retail sales, own use and sales under quote, the producer rebate within the year’s $350,000 or $400,000 cap, and labels 1C and 1D.
