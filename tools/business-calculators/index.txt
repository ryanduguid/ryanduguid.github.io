# Business calculators

Nine calculators, each on its own page with the formula, assumptions and source beside the inputs. Calculations run in your browser; inputs are not saved or sent anywhere.

Find a question and prepare a checklist.

Planning estimates in AUD. Confirm tax treatment, liability amounts and dates separately. Amounts round to the nearest cent, with exact half cents rounded up; loan estimates allow for calculation precision.

Published 11 September 2026. Last reviewed 20 September 2026.

- GST calculator Work out GST, the GST-exclusive amount and the GST-inclusive amount for a wholly taxable supply at 10%, rounded once to cents.
- Business use share calculator Apportion an eligible cost by a supported business use percentage. It does not decide deductibility, depreciation or record sufficiency.
- Margin and markup calculator Gross profit, margin and markup from sales and direct cost, on a consistent GST basis and matching period.
- Break-even sales calculator Whole units and sales required to cover fixed costs from price and variable cost per unit.
- Hourly pricing calculator Required hourly rate before GST from annual costs, target profit and billable hours.
- Budget variance calculator Actual minus budget, the percentage of absolute budget, and whether the difference is favourable or unfavourable.
- Monthly loan payment calculator Fixed monthly payment, first-payment interest and principal, and estimated total interest, with no fees or balloon.
- Staff cost calculator Annual and monthly cost of a staff member from wages, the supplied super amount and other annual costs.
- 13-week cash forecast Weekly closing cash from opening cash, receipts and payments, with one delayed receipt and a funding gap against a buffer. Save, load and export as CSV.
