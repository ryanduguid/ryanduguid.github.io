## How the budget variance calculator works

The budget variance calculator compares one actual figure with one budget figure. Difference = actual − budget. Percentage = difference / absolute budget × 100. A zero budget has no percentage comparison. Positive income differences are favourable; positive cost differences are unfavourable.

No statutory rate or external source is applied. The favourability convention is the usual management-accounting one.

## What the budget variance calculator does not decide

The budget variance calculator does not say why a difference arose or whether it matters. Explain causes from the records.
