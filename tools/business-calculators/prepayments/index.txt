## How the prepaid expenses calculator works

The calculator works out when one prepaid amount, incurred in an income year from 2020-21 to 2026-27, is deductible under Subdivision H of Division 3 of Part III. Excluded expenditure, being less than $1,000, required by a law or court order, or salary or wages, is deductible in full when incurred, as is expenditure for a thing wholly done by the end of the year. The eligible service period starts on the later of the first service day and the day incurred and runs for at most 10 years. A small business entity or an entity with turnover under $50 million that has not chosen section 82KZMD, and an individual’s non-business expenditure, deduct the amount in full when the period is no longer than 12 months and ends by the end of the next income year (the 12-month rule). Otherwise the amount is apportioned by the days of the period in each income year, on the running total of days and rounded to the cent there, so the years add up to the expenditure.

## What the prepaid expenses calculator does not cover

The calculator does not decide whether the expenditure is deductible at all, whether a law or contract requires it, or whether section 82KZME applies to an agreement: you state those facts. It refuses an agreement entered into on or before 11 November 1999, which may carry earlier rules, and expenditure incurred before 1 July 2020. It does not cover a later disposal or change of the agreement, or the other rules for a tax shelter arrangement. Results are planning estimates: check them against your records and the ATO’s guidance.
