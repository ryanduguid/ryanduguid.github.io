BAS due dates for 2026-27

The ATO’s lodgment and payment due dates for monthly and quarterly BAS covering 2026-27, with the dates for lodging online yourself and through a registered BAS agent. Each date was checked at its ATO page on 15 November 2026.

Monthly BAS due dates for 2026-27

A monthly BAS is due for lodgment and payment on the 21st day of the month after the month it covers. The registered agent concession applies only to the December statement.

Quarterly BAS due dates for 2026-27

A quarterly BAS is due on the 28th day of the month after the quarter, except quarter 2, which is due on 28 February. Quarter 2 has no later online or agent date because its due date already includes a one-month extension.

BAS due dates on a weekend or public holiday

The ATO’s due dates page says: ‘If the due date is on a weekend or public holiday, you have until the next business day to lodge and pay.’ The tables show each date as the ATO publishes it and do not move it.

Four dates in the tables fall on a weekend: Saturday 21 November 2026, Sunday 21 February 2027, Sunday 28 February 2027 and Sunday 21 March 2027. None of the weekday dates is a public holiday for the whole of a state or territory on the Payday Super business days calendar. The ATO page does not say which state’s public holidays apply.

Who gets the later BAS dates

The online dates are the ATO’s 2-week lodgment concession. A business that receives and lodges its quarterly activity statement online may get 2 more weeks to lodge and pay for quarters 1, 3 and 4, without applying; if it qualifies, the later date shows on the statement once it generates. It does not apply to monthly statements, monthly GST reporters with quarterly PAYG instalments, quarterly instalment notices (forms R, S and T), head companies’ quarterly PAYG instalments, large business clients with a substituted accounting period or any other statement whose original due date is not the 28th.

The agent dates are the BAS agent lodgment program for 2026-27. They apply to eligible quarterly statements, and to the December monthly statement, lodged through Online services for agents or the Practitioner lodgment service. A statement qualifies once the client’s previous activity statement was lodged electronically; a first statement, or one after a paper lodgment, may be due earlier.

The December monthly date of 21 February 2027 is for business clients with turnover up to $10 million who report GST monthly and lodge electronically through a registered agent. Other monthly statements have no agent concession.

What the BAS tables cover

The tables cover the BAS periods from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027 for businesses that report GST monthly or quarterly. The ATO shows the due date for lodging and paying on each activity statement, so check it there before relying on a date here.

They do not cover:

  • annual GST reporting, due on 31 October, or 28 February when no tax return is required
  • quarter 4 of 2025-26, due on 28 July 2026, or 25 August 2026 through an agent
  • a deferral the ATO grants, including extra time after a natural disaster
