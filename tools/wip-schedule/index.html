Construction WIP: over-billed or under-billed under AASB 15

TheWIPTally reads one row per unit of account and produces a cost-to-cost work-in-progress schedule: earned revenue after AASB 15 para B19 exclusions, constrained unapproved variations, and a contract asset or contract liability per job. Assets and liabilities are never netted. Review aid, not a determination.

Construction month-end still lives or dies on the WIP schedule. The hard part is not the formula. It is applying the same formula every month: strip waste from percent complete without dropping it from margin, book uninstalled materials at cost, constrain unapproved claims, and present each contract as an asset or a liability, never a portfolio net.

This tool is the arithmetic. It is not a second JavaScript calculator. Install the Python engine, point it at a contract CSV, and keep the schedule beside the review pack. Over-time versus point in time, contract combination, claim enforceability, and any onerous-contract provision stay with a person. hardhat-ledger still owns that workflow: it assembles the file and names this engine rather than inventing the numbers.

What this tool does

  • Measures cost-to-cost progress after AASB 15 para B19 exclusions: waste does not accelerate percent complete; uninstalled materials are recognised at cost with zero margin.
  • Constrains unapproved variation estimates by the include ratio you supply, flags the excluded slice, and never treats an uncertified claim as billing or entitlement.
  • Classifies each contract as a contract asset or a contract liability, flags profit fade of 1.00 percentage point or more, and flags a stale estimate to complete after material extra spend.

Limits

This is an educational tool, not tax, legal or financial advice, and using it creates no professional relationship. Outputs are not an accounting opinion, an audit conclusion, or an AASB 15 determination. Confirm the operative compilation at standards.aasb.gov.au before citing a paragraph number, and leave sign-off with a registered practitioner.

Common questions

What is a contract asset versus a contract liability?

Revenue to date less certified billings, per contract. Positive is a contract asset. Negative is a contract liability. The engine never offsets one job against another.

Does wasted cost accelerate percent complete?

No. Para B19(a) amounts are stripped from the progress measure and kept in estimated cost at completion, so they hit margin instead of pulling revenue forward.

Are unapproved variations booked in full?

No. Only the constrained slice (unapproved estimate multiplied by the include ratio) enters transaction price. A bare 25 is refused; use 0.25 or 25%.

Is this an accounting determination?

No. It is a review aid. Over-time versus point in time, whether a claim is enforceable, and any AASB 137 onerous provision stay with the engagement lead.
