## How the employee share scheme discount calculator works

An employee share scheme discount is the market value of the shares or rights when you acquired them less what you paid. Division 83A of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 includes it in your assessable income in one of 3 ways. Most discounts are taxed upfront, in the year you acquired the interest (s 83A-25). If the scheme meets the conditions for the $1,000 reduction and your income for the test is $180,000 or less, s 83A-35 takes up to $1,000 off the upfront discounts that qualify. An eligible start-up concession reduces the amount to nil (s 83A-33).

Tax is deferred (Subdivision 83A-C) where there is a real risk of forfeiture, where up to $5,000 of shares a year are acquired by salary sacrifice, or for rights whose scheme says so. Then the amount is the market value at the deferred taxing point less the cost base, in the year of that point: the earliest of the restrictions ending, 15 years, and for interests acquired before 1 July 2022 the end of your employment, moved to a disposal within 30 days after it. An unlisted right can be valued by the regulations’ tables. The result shows the amounts for question 12 of your return: labels D, E, F and B.

## What the employee share scheme discount calculator does not cover

The calculator does not take interests acquired before 1 July 2015, foreign employment or foreign or temporary residents, or the capital gains tax on a later disposal. It never values a share: enter market values from your employer’s statement or a valuation. The form takes one interest; the WebMCP tool takes up to 50, with forfeited interests and the regulations’ value at a taxing point. Confirm the amounts with your employee share scheme statement and a registered tax agent.
