# Individual tax calculators

Three calculators for one person’s tax year, each on its own page, with every rate and threshold cited to its Act and the ATO’s worked examples among the tested cases. They run in your browser and do not send or save your inputs.

Estimates in AUD for a full income year from 2024-25 to 2026-27, for a single person with no dependants. Each page says what it does not compute. Amounts round to the nearest cent, with exact half cents rounded up.

Published 17 November 2026. Last checked 17 November 2026, the newest of the calculators’ source checks.

## Tax, pay and study loans

- Income tax calculator Tax on taxable income, the low income tax offset and the Medicare levy, for an Australian resident, a foreign resident or a working holiday maker.
- Take-home pay calculator What is left of an annual salary each year, month, fortnight and week after tax, Medicare levy and any study loan repayment, with the employer’s super guarantee.
- Study and training loan calculator The compulsory HELP, VSL, SSL or AASL repayment from repayment income, by the marginal system from 2025-26.

For a business, see the business calculators.
