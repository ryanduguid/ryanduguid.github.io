# Individual tax calculators

Sixteen calculators for one person’s tax, pay and super, with sources for the rates and thresholds and the ATO’s worked examples among the tested cases. They run in your browser and do not send or save your inputs.

Estimates in AUD for one person’s full income year: income tax and study loans from 2020-21 to 2026-27, take-home pay, a sole trader’s set-aside and Division 293 tax from 2021-22, salary packaging from 2022-23, novated leases from 2023-24, the withholding from a 2026-27 pay with a bonus or back payment, and super projected from 2026-27. Each page says what it does not compute. Amounts round to the nearest cent, with exact half cents rounded up.

Published 17 November 2026. Last checked 22 November 2026, the newest of the calculators’ source checks.

## Tax, pay and study loans

- Income tax calculator Tax on taxable income, the offsets, the Medicare levy and surcharge and any study loan repayment, less the tax withheld, for an Australian resident, a foreign resident or a working holiday maker.
- Take-home pay calculator What is left of a salary each year, month, fortnight and week after salary sacrifice to super, tax, levies and any study loan repayment, with the employer’s super guarantee and the PAYG withheld from each pay under that year’s schedules.
- Study and training loan calculator The compulsory HELP, VSL, SSL or AASL repayment from repayment income, by the marginal system from 2025-26.
- Sole trader tax set-aside calculator How much of each invoice and each quarter a sole trader keeps for income tax, the Medicare levy, any study loan repayment and GST, after the small business income tax offset, on top of any wages.
- Lump sum in arrears tax offset calculator The tax offset for back pay received as a lump sum, with the 10% test and the tax the arrears would have cost in each year they accrued.
- Income tests calculator Income for surcharge purposes, Division 293 income, rebate income, repayment income, adjusted taxable income and co-contribution income, from the amounts in one tax return.
- Bonus withholding calculator The PAYG withheld from a 2026-27 pay that includes a bonus, commission or back payment, by the ATO’s Schedule 5 methods, step by step, with the tax the payment adds to the year.
- Salary packaging calculator What packaging saves an employee of a charity, hospital or ambulance service, with the FBT cap, any FBT on the excess and the reportable amount.
- Novated lease calculator A year of a novated car lease against paying from after-tax pay, with the electric car FBT exemption tested condition by condition.
- Work expenses calculator Working from home by the fixed rate against actual cost, car expenses by cents per kilometre or log book, and self-education expenses, 2020-21 to 2026-27.

## Super contributions and retirement

- Division 293 tax calculator The extra 15% tax on concessional super contributions above the $250,000 threshold, after excess contributions and unused cap carried forward, with the room left under it.
- Concessional catch-up calculator The concessional contributions cap with unused cap carried forward from the previous 5 years, earliest year first, and the year each amount lapses.
- Super projection calculator Your super balance at retirement in future and today’s dollars, with every assumption stated and changeable.
- First home super saver calculator The release, withholding and tax offset from an ATO determination, or a contribution ledger estimate with published shortfall interest charge rates.
- Super co-contribution, LISTO and spouse offset calculator The government’s co-contribution and low income super tax offset, and the spouse contribution tax offset, for 2020-21 to 2026-27, with each condition met or not.
- Transfer balance and excess contributions calculator Your transfer balance account and personal cap with proportional indexation, excess transfer balance earnings and tax, the excess concessional contributions charge, and the release of excess non-concessional contributions.

For a business, see the business calculators.
