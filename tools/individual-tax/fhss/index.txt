## Use your ATO determination

In ATO online services through myGov, go to Super, then Manage, then First home saver. Follow the ATO’s steps to check your contributions and request a determination. Enter the 3 amounts from the determination. Enter the release request date for the correct income year. For a completed release, enter the gross actually released and confirm the withholding and any debt offset against your ATO records.

## Estimate the maximum from contributions

The calculator counts up to $15,000 of eligible contributions a year and $50,000 in total ($30,000 for determinations requested before 1 July 2022). It counts contributions earliest first, using non-concessional contributions first in a year that contains both kinds of personal contribution. It treats contributions on the same day as made at the same time and counts the non-concessional ones first. It adds 100% of the non-concessional part and 85% of the concessional part. It compounds daily at the ATO’s published shortfall interest charge rates, from the first day of the contribution month (1 July 2017 for 2017-18 contributions) through the determination date. Earnings round down to whole dollars per original contribution.

In the ATO’s Jill example, this ledger method estimates $7,859 in earnings. The ATO gives $5,190 in GN 2024/1, example 6. The difference remains unresolved. Use the associated earnings on your determination for the actual release.

## How the release is taxed

The assessable amount is the gross released less the non-concessional component, floored at zero. It counts in the income year you requested the release. The 30% tax offset is non-refundable. The ATO can withhold at the estimated marginal rate including Medicare levy less 30 percentage points, or 17% where the Commissioner cannot estimate that rate. The marginal rate option here assumes the ordinary 2% Medicare levy and rounds withholding down to whole dollars. Any Commonwealth debt offset comes out after withholding.

If you do not know the withholding, calculate the assessable amount and offset first. The net release and minimum recontribution need the withholding amount.

The 20% FHSS tax and minimum recontribution figures apply conditionally under the First Home Super Saver Tax Act 2017 and Division 313. Check the contract, notification and recontribution deadlines with the ATO. For determinations made before 15 September 2024, see Treasury Laws Amendment (2023 Measures No. 3) Act 2023, Schedule 4, items 28 and 30. For an item 30 release, enter the original determination date and the amounts the ATO confirmed.

Confirm eligibility, contribution classification, any transitional treatment and the amount to report with the ATO or a registered tax agent. This calculator works the release figures you enter.
