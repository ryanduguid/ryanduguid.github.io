## How the lodgment test works

Each year the Commissioner makes an instrument under s 161 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 that says who must lodge a tax return. The calculator applies the year’s instrument to one person: it lists each condition you ticked, works out the ones that turn on an amount, and then checks the exceptions you answered. You must lodge if any condition is met and no exception that names it applies. Tax withheld counts unless it was withheld only from a payment the instrument excludes, such as dividends, interest or royalties paid to a non-resident.

The amounts it works out are the $18,200 tax-free threshold for a resident all year; for a part-year resident, the lesser of $18,200 and $13,464 plus $395 for each month resident; $416 for a person under 18 with income other than from work; $18,200 of dividends and franking credits; and, for a foreign resident with a study or training loan debt, the year’s repayment income threshold. A non-resident with income taxable in Australia must lodge, unless the only such income is dividends, interest, royalties, managed investment trust payments or labour mobility program payments taxed by withholding.

The exceptions are a working holiday maker with no other assessable income and taxable income under $45,001; a person who received a payment the instrument lists, with no other assessable income or taxable income under the year’s limit; and a person eligible for the seniors and pensioners tax offset with rebate income under the year’s limit. The due date shown is 31 October after the year, for a person who lodges without a registered tax agent.

## What the lodgment test does not cover

The calculator covers one individual’s own return for 2020-21 to 2025-26. It does not cover a return as a trustee, partner, agent or ship’s master, a company or other full self-assessment taxpayer, or a substituted accounting period. No instrument for 2026-27 had been made when the calculator was built, so that year is refused. It does not decide your residency or test the conditions it takes as your answers, and it does not apply the separate instrument for parents liable for or entitled to child support: a parent the main instrument does not require to lodge is told to check that one. It does not say whether lodging would be worthwhile: you may still lodge, for example to get a refund of tax withheld, and the ATO may ask you to lodge. A registered tax agent’s lodgment program may allow a later date than 31 October.
