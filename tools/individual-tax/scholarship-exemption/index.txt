## How the scholarship exemption check works

A scholarship, bursary, educational allowance or educational assistance paid to a full-time student at a school, college or university is exempt income under item 2.1A of the table in s 51-10, unless s 51-35 takes it out. The check asks the 3 exceptions that turn on the payment’s terms: a condition that you become or stay the payer’s employee, a condition that you enter into or keep a contract wholly or principally for your labour, and a scholarship not provided principally for educational purposes. If every answer allows it, the payment is exempt and you leave it out of your tax return.

## What the scholarship exemption check does not cover

The check refuses the Commonwealth payments s 51-35 excludes (assistance for secondary education or isolated children, education or training payments such as Austudy, ABSTUDY and Youth Allowance, and the education entry payment), which other provisions may exempt in whole or part. It also refuses friendly society scholarship plans, which can be taxable. It does not test a prize or award, decide whether you are a full-time student, or work out tax or withholding on a payment that is not exempt. Confirm your answers with the provider, the ATO or a registered tax agent.
