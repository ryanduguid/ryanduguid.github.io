Using these functions

Each function is a native Excel LAMBDA in the free Ozzit workbook. It needs Microsoft 365 or Excel 2024 or later. Enter a call in a cell of the workbook, or copy the definition from its name manager into your own model. Leave room for any spilled result.

The text below is the help each function carries in the v3.5.0 workbook. The workbook remains the authority, and a calculation does not decide an accounting treatment. Download the workbook and check its hash.

oz.CurrentRatioλ

oz.CurrentRatioλ( Assets, Liabilities)

Measures a company’s ability to pay short-term obligations (Current Assets/Liabilities)

Parameters:

Assets (required)
Current Assets. Includes:
  • Cash
  • Accounts receivable
  • Inventory
  • Vendor non-trade receivables
  • Assets expected to be liquidated in the current year
  • Marketable securities
  • PPE only when it meets current-asset classification criteria
Liabilities (required)
Current Liabilities. Includes:
  • Accounts payable
  • Wages
  • Taxes payable
  • Short-term debts
  • Current portion of long-term debt

oz.ATOTurnoverλ

oz.ATOTurnoverλ(OtherSales, TotalBusinessIncome)

Turnover as the ATO small business benchmarks measure it: other sales unless under half of total business income

Turnover excluding GST, the denominator of every ATO tax return benchmark ratio.

Note: Returns OtherSales, unless it is zero, blank or less than half of TotalBusinessIncome, when it returns TotalBusinessIncome instead. That is the ATO's rule. OtherSales is the other sales of goods and services amount on the return. Where total business income is blank or incomplete, the ATO adds up the business income items it lists for the return type: pass that total as TotalBusinessIncome. Pass both amounts excluding GST. Text in either argument returns #VALUE!. Arithmetic only: the reviewer decides what belongs in each amount. ATO benchmark ratio calculations checked 19 October 2026.

Parameters:

OtherSales (required)
Other sales of goods and services, excluding GST.
TotalBusinessIncome (required)
Total business income, excluding GST.
=oz.ATOTurnoverλ(480000, 500000)

The workbook help shows 480000.

=oz.ATOTurnoverλ(200000, 500000)

The workbook help shows 500000.

oz.ATOBenchmarkRatioλ(Amount, Turnover)

ATO tax return benchmark ratio: an amount divided by turnover, as for rent or motor vehicle expenses to turnover
