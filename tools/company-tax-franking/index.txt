# What company tax rate applies, and is the franking account in order?

A company gets the lower tax rate only if it is a base rate entity: aggregated turnover under the year's threshold, and no more than 80% of assessable income being base rate entity passive income. Get that test wrong and the error carries straight into the rate used to frank a dividend. This tool runs the test, tracks the franking account, and checks the Division 203 benchmark rule.

Reference release the-exchequer-tally/v0.1.3, tagged source on GitHub.

Published 24 August 2026. Last reviewed 11 September 2026.

This tool runs the base rate entity test and tracks a franking account ledger covering PAYG instalments, company tax paid, dividends paid and received, and refunds. It works out franking deficit tax and the 30% offset reduction penalty where a deficit exceeds 10% of annual credits. It also checks distributions in a franking period against the Division 203 benchmark rule for over-franking tax and franking debit shortfalls.

It builds the statement fields a dividend distribution statement needs, too. That is a review helper for the statement, not a lodgement or a compliance certificate.

## What this tool does

- Tests whether a company is a base rate entity for a given financial year, against the aggregated turnover threshold and the 80% passive income test.
- Tracks a franking account ledger and works out franking deficit tax and the 30% offset reduction penalty where the deficit exceeds 10% of annual credits.
- Checks distributions in a franking period against the Division 203 benchmark rule, for over-franking tax and franking debit shortfalls.

## Limits

Nothing this engine produces is tax, legal, or financial advice, an assessment or a determination. Outputs are review aids: confirm every rate, threshold, and consequence against the current law and the entity's facts before acting, and leave lodgement decisions with a registered practitioner.

## Fabricated worked example

For the income year ending in 2025, use fabricated aggregated turnover of $4,500,000, assessable income of $800,000, and base rate entity passive income of $120,000. The caller must establish those classifications before running the test.

```
the-exchequer-tally bre-test --fy 2025 --turnover 4500000 --assessable 800000 --passive 120000
```

```
Passive Income Ratio:    15.00% (<= 80%: True)
Base Rate Entity:        True
Applicable Tax Rate:     25.0%
```

### Try a refusal

Change --fy 2025 to --fy 2017. Release 0.1.3 has no bundled rate table for that year. It exits 2 and returns no rate.

This verifies the published command's behaviour on fabricated inputs. It does not determine the maximum franking rate, which uses prior-year facts, or replace review of the company's circumstances.

## Common questions

### What makes a company a base rate entity?

Two tests, both of which must hold for the income year: aggregated turnover below that year's threshold, and no more than 80% of assessable income being base rate entity passive income.

### What is franking deficit tax?

Tax payable where a franking account ends the year in deficit. Where the deficit exceeds 10% of the credits arising in the year, the offset may be reduced by 30%, but only for the part of the liability that relates to franking debits arising under the provisions listed in section 205-70(8); the debit types and any applicable exceptions need checking before the reduction is applied.

### What is the benchmark rule?

Under Division 203, a company must frank all distributions in a franking period to the same extent. Departing from the benchmark franking percentage produces over-franking tax or a franking debit shortfall.
