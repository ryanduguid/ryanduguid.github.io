Subcontract ledger workflows for construction and mining

This is not a standalone calculator. It is a set of Claude Code skills, so you need an AI coding agent, such as Claude Code, already set up before any of it does anything. Without one, these skills will not run.

With an agent in place, the skills cover the contracting core: progress claim preparation, retention schedules, work in progress and over-and-under billing under AASB 15, and contract cost tracking. For mining services and earthmoving specifically, they cover fuel tax credit claims, the Coal LSL levy, payroll tax on contractors, and plant and equipment costing.

Each skill encodes the workflow, the steps, the tie-outs, the exceptions to chase, rather than hard-coding rates and thresholds that go stale and differ by state; it sends the agent back to the primary source instead. Two things it deliberately will not do: decide whether a contract is covered by a security of payment Act, or conclude that a worker is a contractor rather than an employee. Both stay with a person.

What this tool does

  • The contracting core: progress claim preparation, retention schedules, work in progress and over-and-under billing under AASB 15, and contract cost tracking.
  • Mining services and earthmoving: fuel tax credit claims, the Coal LSL levy, payroll tax on contractors, and plant and equipment costing.
  • Paying subcontractors: super guarantee under the extended definition, TPAR, and a shared reference skill for the exports these workflows need.

Limits

These skills prepare and check. They do not serve a payment claim, lodge a return, or sign anything. Contract interpretation, eligibility conclusions and professional judgement stay with a person. Nothing here is tax, accounting or legal advice.
