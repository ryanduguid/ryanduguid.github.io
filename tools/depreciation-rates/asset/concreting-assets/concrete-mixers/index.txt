# Concreting assets: Concrete mixers (Table B)

‘Concreting assets: Concrete mixers’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 4 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2008.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Table | Table B (asset categories), letter C
