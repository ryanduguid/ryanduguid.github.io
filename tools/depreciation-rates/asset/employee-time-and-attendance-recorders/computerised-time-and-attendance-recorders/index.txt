# Employee time and attendance recorders …: Computerised time and attendance recorders …

‘Employee time and attendance recorders …: Computerised time and attendance recorders …’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 10 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2013.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Computerised time and attendance recorders (including fingerprint and face recognition systems and swipe card digital time clock systems)
Under | Employee time and attendance recorders (including bundy clocks, time clocks etc):
Table | Table B (asset categories), letter E
Effective life | 10 years
Prime cost rate | 10.00% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 20.00% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2013
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

Use this Table B row if your asset matches its description and no Table A row covers the asset or its function in your industry (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2013. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
