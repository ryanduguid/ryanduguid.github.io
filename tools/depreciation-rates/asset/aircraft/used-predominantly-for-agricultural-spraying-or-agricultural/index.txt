# Aeroplanes: Used predominantly for agricultural spraying or agricultural dusting (Table B)

‘Aeroplanes: Used predominantly for agricultural spraying or agricultural dusting’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 10 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2002.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

Use this Table B row if your asset matches its description and no Table A row covers the asset or its function in your industry (subsection 6(3)).
