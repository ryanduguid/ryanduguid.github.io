# Swimming pool assets: Chlorinators (Table B)

‘Swimming pool assets: Chlorinators’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 12 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2004.
