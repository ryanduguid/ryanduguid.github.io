# Distribution lines: Overhead …

‘Distribution lines: Overhead …’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 45 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2014.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Overhead (incorporating poles - concrete, wood, steel or stobie - and electrical equipment the responsibility of the private landholder such as conductors, cross arms etc)
Under | Power supply assets: / Private electricity line assets (where used for a specified purpose): / Distribution lines:
Table | Table B (asset categories), letter P
Effective life | 45 years
Prime cost rate | 2.22% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 4.44% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2014
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

Use this Table B row if your asset matches its description and no Table A row covers the asset or its function in your industry (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘incorporating’, the items it lists are components of the one asset, not separate assets (paragraph 4 of the guide).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2014. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
