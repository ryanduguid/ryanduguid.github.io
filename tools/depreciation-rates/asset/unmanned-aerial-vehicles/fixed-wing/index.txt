# Unmanned aerial vehicles …: Fixed wing (Table B)

‘Unmanned aerial vehicles …: Fixed wing’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 3 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2015.

## Reading this row

Table B applies where no row of Table A for the industry the asset is used in describes it or its function, and the asset corresponds to this row (subsection 6(3) of the determination).

Where ‘fixed’ describes how an asset is attached, it means an item annexed or attached by any means, such as screws, nails, bolts, glue, adhesive, grout or cement, other than merely for temporary stability (paragraph 8 of the guide).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2015. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
