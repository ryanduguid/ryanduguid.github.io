# Computers and computer equipment: Raised floors

‘Computers and computer equipment: Raised floors’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 25 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2017.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Table | Table B (asset categories), letter C
