# Advertising signs: LED advertising screens (used in office tower foyers etc)

‘Advertising signs: LED advertising screens (used in office tower foyers etc)’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 5 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2015.
