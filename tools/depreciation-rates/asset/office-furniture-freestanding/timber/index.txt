# Bookcases: Timber

‘Bookcases: Timber’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2005.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.
