Plumbing fixtures and fittings … provided mainly for employees and/or children of employees of an entity carrying on a business for the purpose of producing assessable income: Generally …

‘Plumbing fixtures and fittings … provided mainly for employees and/or children of employees of an entity carrying on a business for the purpose of producing assessable income: Generally …’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 20 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed on 1 July 2015.

Reading this row

Table B applies where no row of Table A for the industry the asset is used in describes it or its function, and the asset corresponds to this row (subsection 6(3) of the determination).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2015. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
