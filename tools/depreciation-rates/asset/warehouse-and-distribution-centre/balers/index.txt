# Warehouse and distribution centre equipment and machines: Balers (Table B)

‘Warehouse and distribution centre equipment and machines: Balers’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 15 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2011.
