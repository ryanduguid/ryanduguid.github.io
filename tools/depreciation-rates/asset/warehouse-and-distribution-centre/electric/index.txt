# Waste compactors …: Electric (Table B)

‘Waste compactors …: Electric’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2011.
