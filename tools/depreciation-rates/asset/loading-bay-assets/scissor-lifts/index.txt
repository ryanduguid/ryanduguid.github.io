# Loading bay assets: Scissor lifts

‘Loading bay assets: Scissor lifts’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2005.
