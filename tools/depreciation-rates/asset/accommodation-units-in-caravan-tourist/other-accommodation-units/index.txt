# Accommodation units in caravan/tourist parks being articles, not fixtures, and used for a specified purpose: Other accommodation units (e.g. manufactured homes)

‘Accommodation units in caravan/tourist parks being articles, not fixtures, and used for a specified purpose: Other accommodation units (e.g. manufactured homes)’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 30 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed on 1 July 2015.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Other accommodation units (e.g. manufactured homes)
Under | Accommodation units in caravan/tourist parks being articles, not fixtures, and used for a specified purpose:
Table | Table B (asset categories), letter A
Effective life | 30 years
Prime cost rate | 3.33% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 6.67% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2015
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

Use this Table B row if your asset matches its description and no Table A row covers the asset or its function in your industry (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2015. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
