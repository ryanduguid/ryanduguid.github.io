# Office machines and equipment: Trolleys

‘Office machines and equipment: Trolleys’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2005.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 21 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

The determination is the Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Trolleys
Under | Office machines and equipment:
Table | Table B (asset categories), letter O
Effective life | 15 years
Prime cost rate | 6.67% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 13.33% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2005
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

Table B applies where no row of Table A for the industry the asset is used in describes it or its function, and the asset corresponds to this row (subsection 6(3) of the determination).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2005. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
