# Office machines and equipment: Electronic whiteboards

‘Office machines and equipment: Electronic whiteboards’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 6 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2001.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.
