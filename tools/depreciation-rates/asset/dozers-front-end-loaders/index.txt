# Dozers/front end loaders (Table B)

‘Dozers/front end loaders’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 9 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2002.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 26 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates from F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Dozers/front end loaders
Table | Table B (asset categories), letter D
Effective life | 9 years
Prime cost rate | 11.11% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 22.22% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2002
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

Table B applies where no row of Table A for the industry the asset is used in describes it or its function, and the asset corresponds to this row (subsection 6(3) of the determination).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2002. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
