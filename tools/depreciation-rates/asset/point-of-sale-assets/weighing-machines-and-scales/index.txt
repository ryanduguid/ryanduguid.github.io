# Point of sale assets: Weighing machines and scales …

‘Point of sale assets: Weighing machines and scales …’ in Table B, which applies where no Table A row applies to the asset in its industry, has an effective life of 10 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2005.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 26 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates from F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Weighing machines and scales (including weigh labelling machines)
Under | Point of sale assets:
Table | Table B (asset categories), letter P
Effective life | 10 years
Prime cost rate | 10.00% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 20.00% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2005
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

Table B applies where no row of Table A for the industry the asset is used in describes it or its function, and the asset corresponds to this row (subsection 6(3) of the determination).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2005. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
