# Play area assets: Pet enclosures and structures, freestanding …

‘Play area assets: Pet enclosures and structures, freestanding …’, for an asset used in Child care services, has an effective life of 10 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2020.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Reading this row

In Child care services, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

Where ‘freestanding’ describes an asset, it means an item designed to be portable or movable, attached to the premises only for temporary stability (paragraph 7 of the guide).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2020. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
