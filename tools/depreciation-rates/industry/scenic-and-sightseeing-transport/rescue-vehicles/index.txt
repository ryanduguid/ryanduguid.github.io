# Monocable circulating detachable, reversible and fixed ropeway assets operated in non-snowfield areas: Rescue vehicles

‘Monocable circulating detachable, reversible and fixed ropeway assets operated in non-snowfield areas: Rescue vehicles’, for an asset used in Scenic and sightseeing transport, has an effective life of 30 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2009.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Reading this row

In Scenic and sightseeing transport, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

Where ‘fixed’ describes how an asset is attached, it means an item annexed or attached by any means, such as screws, nails, bolts, glue, adhesive, grout or cement, other than merely for temporary stability (paragraph 8 of the guide).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2009. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
