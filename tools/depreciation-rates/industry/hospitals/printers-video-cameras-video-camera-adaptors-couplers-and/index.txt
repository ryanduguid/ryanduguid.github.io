‘Endoscopic camera systems: Printers, video cameras, video camera adaptors, couplers and heads, video image capture systems and video processors’, for an asset used in Hospitals, has an effective life of 5 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2003.

## Reading this row

In Hospitals, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).
