# Dimensioning machines

‘Dimensioning machines’, for an asset used in WHOLESALE TRADE, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2019.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Dimensioning machines
Table | Table A (industry categories), WHOLESALE TRADE, rows for the whole division, ANZSIC 33110 to 38000
Effective life | 15 years
Prime cost rate | 6.67% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 13.33% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2019
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

In WHOLESALE TRADE, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2019. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
