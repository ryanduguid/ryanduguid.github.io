# Magnetic separation assets: LIMS (low intensity magnetic separators)

‘Magnetic separation assets: LIMS (low intensity magnetic separators)’, for an asset used in Iron ore mining, has an effective life of 20 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2006.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Reading this row

In Iron ore mining, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 January 2006. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
