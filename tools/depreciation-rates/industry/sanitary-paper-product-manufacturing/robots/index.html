Packaging assets: Robots …

‘Packaging assets: Robots …’, for an asset used in Sanitary paper product manufacturing, has an effective life of 10 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2013.

Reading this row

In Sanitary paper product manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2013. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
