# Cold storage assets: Chillers (incorporating pneumatic gates)

‘Cold storage assets: Chillers (incorporating pneumatic gates)’, for an asset used in Meat processing, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2013.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Reading this row

In Meat processing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘incorporating’, the items it lists are components of the one asset, not separate assets (paragraph 4 of the guide).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2013. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
