# Boning room assets: Sausage filling machines

‘Boning room assets: Sausage filling machines’, for an asset used in Meat processing, has an effective life of 10 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2013.
