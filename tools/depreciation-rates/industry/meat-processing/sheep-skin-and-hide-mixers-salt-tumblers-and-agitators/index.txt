# Hide preparation assets: Sheep skin and hide mixers, salt tumblers and agitators

‘Hide preparation assets: Sheep skin and hide mixers, salt tumblers and agitators’, for an asset used in Meat processing, has an effective life of 6 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2013.
