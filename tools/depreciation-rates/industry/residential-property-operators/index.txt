# Residential property operators

167 effective lives in Table A of the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination for Residential property operators.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 14 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

The determination notes for this heading: where the terms ‘freestanding’ and ‘fixed’ are used in entries for residential property operators, they have the following meaning. Freestanding – items designed to be portable or movable. Any attachment to the premises is only for the item’s temporary stability. Fixed – annexed or attached by any means, for example screws, nails, bolts, glue, adhesive, grout or cement, but not merely for temporary stability.
