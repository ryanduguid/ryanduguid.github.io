# Infrastructure support assets: Coal preparation facility framework/structure

‘Infrastructure support assets: Coal preparation facility framework/structure’, for an asset used in Coal mining, has an effective life of 40 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2003.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 23 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

The determination is the Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Coal preparation facility framework/structure
Under | Infrastructure support assets:
Table | Table A (industry categories), MINING / Coal mining, ANZSIC 06000
Effective life | 40 years
Prime cost rate | 2.50% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 5.00% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2003
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Coal mining takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2003. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
