# Sheet metal tanks manufacturing assets: Racking

‘Sheet metal tanks manufacturing assets: Racking’, for an asset used in Boiler, tank and other heavy gauge metal container manufacturing, has an effective life of 20 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2012.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Table | Table A (industry categories), MANUFACTURING / Boiler, tank and other heavy gauge metal container manufacturing, ANZSIC 22310
Effective life | 20 years
Prime cost rate | 5.00% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 10.00% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2012
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

In Boiler, tank and other heavy gauge metal container manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2012. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
