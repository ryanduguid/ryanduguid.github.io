# Sheet metal tanks manufacturing assets: Drilling machines (including bench and radial drills)

‘Sheet metal tanks manufacturing assets: Drilling machines (including bench and radial drills)’, for an asset used in Boiler, tank and other heavy gauge metal container manufacturing, has an effective life of 5 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2012.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 23 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

The determination is the Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Boiler, tank and other heavy gauge metal container manufacturing takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2012. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
