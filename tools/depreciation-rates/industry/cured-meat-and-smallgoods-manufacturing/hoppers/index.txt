# Ancillary assets: Hoppers

‘Ancillary assets: Hoppers’, for an asset used in Cured meat and smallgoods manufacturing, has an effective life of 12 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2016.

## Reading this row

In Cured meat and smallgoods manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2016. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
