# Retail bread, biscuit, cake and pastry baking assets: Planetary mixers

‘Retail bread, biscuit, cake and pastry baking assets: Planetary mixers’, for an asset used in Bakery product manufacturing (non-factory based), has an effective life of 7 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2002.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

In Bakery product manufacturing (non-factory based), this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 January 2002. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
