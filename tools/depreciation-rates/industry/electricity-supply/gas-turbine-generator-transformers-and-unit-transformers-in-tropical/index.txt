# Gas turbine: Generator transformers and unit transformers in tropical areas

‘Gas turbine: Generator transformers and unit transformers in tropical areas’, for an asset used in Electricity supply, has an effective life of 25 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2002.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 21 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

The determination is the Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Generator transformers and unit transformers in tropical areas
Under | Electricity generation: / Power generators: / Gas turbine:
Table | Table A (industry categories), ELECTRICITY, GAS, WATER AND WASTE SERVICES / Electricity supply, ANZSIC 26110 to 26400
Effective life | 25 years
Prime cost rate | 4.00% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 8.00% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 January 2002
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Electricity supply takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2002. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
