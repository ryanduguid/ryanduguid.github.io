# Power station civil and structural works: Cooling towers (concrete or timber)

‘Power station civil and structural works: Cooling towers (concrete or timber)’, for an asset used in Electricity supply, has an effective life of 30 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2002.
