# Hydro-electric: On-site switchyards with gas insulated switchgear

‘Hydro-electric: On-site switchyards with gas insulated switchgear’, for an asset used in Electricity supply, has an effective life of 35 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2002.

## Reading this row

In Electricity supply, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).
