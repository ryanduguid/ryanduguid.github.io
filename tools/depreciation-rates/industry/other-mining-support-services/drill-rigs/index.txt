# Drilling assets: Drill rigs

‘Drilling assets: Drill rigs’, for an asset used in Other mining support services, has an effective life of 10 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2018.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Drill rigs
Under | Gas and oil mining support services (excluding offshore services): / Drilling assets:
Table | Table A (industry categories), MINING / Other mining support services, ANZSIC 10900
Effective life | 10 years
Prime cost rate | 10.00% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 20.00% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2018
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

In Other mining support services, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2018. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
