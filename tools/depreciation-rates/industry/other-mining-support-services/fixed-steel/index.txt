# Cement silos: Fixed steel

‘Cement silos: Fixed steel’, for an asset used in Other mining support services, has an effective life of 30 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2018.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 26 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Other mining support services takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

Where ‘fixed’ describes how an asset is attached, it means an item annexed or attached by any means, such as screws, nails, bolts, glue, adhesive, grout or cement, other than merely for temporary stability (paragraph 8 of the guide).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2018. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
