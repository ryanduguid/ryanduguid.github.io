# Motor vehicles and trailers: Trailers having a gross vehicle mass greater than 4.5 tonnes (Road transport)

‘Motor vehicles and trailers: Trailers having a gross vehicle mass greater than 4.5 tonnes’, for an asset used in Road transport, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2005.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Trailers having a gross vehicle mass greater than 4.5 tonnes
Under | Motor vehicles and trailers:
Table | Table A (industry categories), TRANSPORT, POSTAL AND WAREHOUSING / Road transport, ANZSIC 46100 to 46239
Effective life | 15 years
Prime cost rate | 6.67% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 13.33% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 January 2005
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.
Capped life | Marked #: a capped life is available under section 40-102. See capped lives.

## Reading this row

In Road transport, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 January 2005. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
