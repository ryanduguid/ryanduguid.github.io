# Fruit juice and fruit juice drink manufacturing assets / Filling and sealing assets: Filling machines … (Fruit and vegetable processing)

‘Filling and sealing assets: Filling machines …’, for an asset used in Fruit and vegetable processing, has an effective life of 20 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2010.

## Reading this row

In Fruit and vegetable processing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2010. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
