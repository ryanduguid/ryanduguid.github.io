# Support assets: Air compression assets … (Fruit and vegetable processing)

‘Support assets: Air compression assets …’, for an asset used in Fruit and vegetable processing, has an effective life of 10 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2018.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

Effective life | 10 years

Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

In Fruit and vegetable processing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2018. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
