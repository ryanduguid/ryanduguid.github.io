# Jack hammers: Electric (CONSTRUCTION)

‘Jack hammers: Electric’, for an asset used in CONSTRUCTION, has an effective life of 3 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2008.
