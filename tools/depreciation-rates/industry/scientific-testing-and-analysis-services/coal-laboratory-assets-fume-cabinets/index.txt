# Coal laboratory assets / Laboratory analytical equipment and instruments: Fume cabinets (Scientific testing and analysis services)

‘Laboratory analytical equipment and instruments: Fume cabinets’, for an asset used in Scientific testing and analysis services, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2018.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Reading this row

In Scientific testing and analysis services, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).
