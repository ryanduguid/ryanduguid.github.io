# Glass handling and storage assets: Racks …

‘Glass handling and storage assets: Racks …’, for an asset used in Glass and glass product manufacturing, has an effective life of 20 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2009.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Racks (including A-frame racks, hydraulic concertina racks and freefall racks)
Under | Glass product manufacturing assets: / Glass handling and storage assets:
Table | Table A (industry categories), MANUFACTURING / Glass and glass product manufacturing, ANZSIC 20100
Effective life | 20 years
Prime cost rate | 5.00% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 10.00% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2009
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

In Glass and glass product manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2009. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
