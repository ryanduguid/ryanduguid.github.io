# Alumina manufacturing …: Filtration assets for hydrate and slurry …

‘Alumina manufacturing …: Filtration assets for hydrate and slurry …’, for an asset used in Alumina production, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2003.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 26 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Source Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Filtration assets for hydrate and slurry (including filters used for clarification of liquor and filters used for coarse hydrate)
Under | Alumina manufacturing (including bauxite refining and calcined alumina manufacturing):
Table | Table A (industry categories), MANUFACTURING / Alumina production, ANZSIC 21310
Effective life | 15 years
Prime cost rate | 6.67% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 13.33% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 January 2003
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Alumina production takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2003. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
