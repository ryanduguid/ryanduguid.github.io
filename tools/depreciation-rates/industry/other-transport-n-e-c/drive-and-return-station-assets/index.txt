# Monocable circulating fixed grip and detachable ropeway assets operated in snowfield areas …: Drive and return station assets …

‘Monocable circulating fixed grip and detachable ropeway assets operated in snowfield areas …: Drive and return station assets …’, for an asset used in Other transport n.e.c., has an effective life of 20 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2009.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 15 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

The determination is the Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Drive and return station assets (including braking systems, drive systems, gear boxes, motors, variable speed drives (VSDs) and tensioning systems)
Under | Monocable circulating fixed grip and detachable ropeway assets operated in snowfield areas (including double, triple and quad chair lifts, T-bar, poma and surface lifts):
Table | Table A (industry categories), TRANSPORT, POSTAL AND WAREHOUSING / Other transport n.e.c., ANZSIC 50290
Effective life | 20 years
Prime cost rate | 5.00% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 10.00% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2009
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Other transport n.e.c. takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

Where ‘fixed’ describes how an asset is attached, it means an item annexed or attached by any means, such as screws, nails, bolts, glue, adhesive, grout or cement, other than merely for temporary stability (paragraph 8 of the guide).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2009. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
