# Sewerage control systems assets: Dissolved oxygen probes, level sensors, transmitters and meters

‘Sewerage control systems assets: Dissolved oxygen probes, level sensors, transmitters and meters’, for an asset used in Sewerage and drainage services, has an effective life of 10 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2005.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 26 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates from F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Under | Sewerage control systems assets:
Table | Table A (industry categories), ELECTRICITY, GAS, WATER AND WASTE SERVICES / Sewerage and drainage services, ANZSIC 28120

Diminishing value rate | 20.00% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)

Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Sewerage and drainage services takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2005. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
