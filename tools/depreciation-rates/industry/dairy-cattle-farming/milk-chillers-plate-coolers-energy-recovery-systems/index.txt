# Robotic milking system assets: Milk chillers/plate coolers/energy recovery systems

‘Robotic milking system assets: Milk chillers/plate coolers/energy recovery systems’, for an asset used in Dairy cattle farming, has an effective life of 10 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2016.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Reading this row

In Dairy cattle farming, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2016. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
