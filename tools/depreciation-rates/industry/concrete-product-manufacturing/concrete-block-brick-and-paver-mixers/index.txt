# Batching and mixing assets: Mixers

‘Batching and mixing assets: Mixers’, for an asset used in Concrete product manufacturing, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2011.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Mixers
Under | Concrete block, brick and paver manufacturing assets: / Batching and mixing assets:
Table | Table A (industry categories), MANUFACTURING / Concrete product manufacturing, ANZSIC 20340
Effective life | 15 years
Prime cost rate | 6.67% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 13.33% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2011
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

In Concrete product manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2011. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
