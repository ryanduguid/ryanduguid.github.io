# Ancillary and support assets: Ammonia gas detectors

‘Ancillary and support assets: Ammonia gas detectors’, for an asset used in Dairy product manufacturing, has an effective life of 12 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2018.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Reading this row

In Dairy product manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2018. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
