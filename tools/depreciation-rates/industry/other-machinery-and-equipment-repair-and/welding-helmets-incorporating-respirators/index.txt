# Welding assets: Welding helmets incorporating respirators

‘Welding assets: Welding helmets incorporating respirators’, for an asset used in Other machinery and equipment repair and maintenance, has an effective life of 3 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2015.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 14 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

The determination is the Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Welding helmets incorporating respirators
Under | Agriculture, construction and mining heavy machinery and equipment repair and maintenance assets: / Workshop assets: / Welding assets:

Prime cost rate | 33.33% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)

Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2015
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Other machinery and equipment repair and maintenance takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘incorporating’, the items it lists are components of the one asset, not separate assets (paragraph 4 of the guide).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2015. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
