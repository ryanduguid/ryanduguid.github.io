# Workshop assets: Guillotine shears

‘Workshop assets: Guillotine shears’, for an asset used in Other machinery and equipment repair and maintenance, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2015.
