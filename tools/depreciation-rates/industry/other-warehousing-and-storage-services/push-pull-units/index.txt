# Forklift attachments: Push pull units (Other warehousing and storage services)

‘Forklift attachments: Push pull units’, for an asset used in Other warehousing and storage services, has an effective life of 11 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2011.

## Reading this row

In Other warehousing and storage services, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2011. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
