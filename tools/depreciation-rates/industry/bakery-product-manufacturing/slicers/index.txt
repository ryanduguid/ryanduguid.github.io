# Baking assets used by large-scale manufacturers of biscuits, bread, cakes, pastries and pies: Slicers …

‘Baking assets used by large-scale manufacturers of biscuits, bread, cakes, pastries and pies: Slicers …’, for an asset used in Bakery product manufacturing, has an effective life of 10 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2002.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 21 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

The determination is the Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Bakery product manufacturing takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2002. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
