# Pulp process assets: Other assets …

‘Pulp process assets: Other assets …’, for an asset used in Pulp, paper and converted paper product manufacturing, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2002.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

In Pulp, paper and converted paper product manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

This life was last determined on 1 January 2002. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
