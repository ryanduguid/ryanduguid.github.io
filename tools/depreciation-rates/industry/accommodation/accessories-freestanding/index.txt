# Bathroom assets: Accessories, freestanding … (Accommodation)

‘Bathroom assets: Accessories, freestanding …’, for an asset used in Accommodation, has an effective life of 1 year under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2005.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 14 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

The determination is the Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

Under | Guestroom assets: / Bathroom assets:

Effective life | 1 year
Prime cost rate | 100.00% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)

Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2005
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Accommodation takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

Where ‘freestanding’ describes an asset, it means an item designed to be portable or movable, attached to the premises only for temporary stability (paragraph 7 of the guide).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2005. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
