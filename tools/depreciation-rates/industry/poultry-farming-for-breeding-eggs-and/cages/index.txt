# Cages (for egg layers)

‘Cages (for egg layers)’, for an asset used in Poultry farming for breeding, eggs and meat, has an effective life of 20 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2007.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Table | Table A (industry categories), AGRICULTURE, FORESTRY AND FISHING / Poultry farming for breeding, eggs and meat, ANZSIC 01710 to 01720
Effective life | 20 years
Prime cost rate | 5.00% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)

Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

In Poultry farming for breeding, eggs and meat, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 January 2007. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
