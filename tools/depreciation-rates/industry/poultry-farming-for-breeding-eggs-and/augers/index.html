Ancillary equipment: Augers

‘Ancillary equipment: Augers’, for an asset used in Poultry farming for breeding, eggs and meat, has an effective life of 10 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2007.

Reading this row

In Poultry farming for breeding, eggs and meat, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 January 2007. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
