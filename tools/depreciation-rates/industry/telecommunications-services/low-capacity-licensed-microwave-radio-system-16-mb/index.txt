# Microwave radio system …: Low capacity licensed microwave radio system < 16 Mb

‘Microwave radio system …: Low capacity licensed microwave radio system < 16 Mb’, for an asset used in Telecommunications services, has an effective life of 5 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2004.

## Reading this row

In Telecommunications services, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2004. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
