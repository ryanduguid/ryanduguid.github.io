# Satellite communication assets: Communications satellites (geosynchronous orbit)

‘Satellite communication assets: Communications satellites (geosynchronous orbit)’, for an asset used in Telecommunications services, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2005.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Communications satellites (geosynchronous orbit)
Under | Satellite communication assets:
Table | Table A (industry categories), INFORMATION MEDIA AND TELECOMMUNICATIONS / Telecommunications services, ANZSIC 58010 to 58090
Effective life | 15 years
Prime cost rate | 6.67% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 13.33% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 January 2005
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

In Telecommunications services, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 January 2005. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
