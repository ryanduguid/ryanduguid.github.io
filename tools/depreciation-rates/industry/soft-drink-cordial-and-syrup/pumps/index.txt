# Support assets: Pumps (Soft drink, cordial and syrup manufacturing)

‘Support assets: Pumps’, for an asset used in Soft drink, cordial and syrup manufacturing, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2009.

## Reading this row

In Soft drink, cordial and syrup manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).
