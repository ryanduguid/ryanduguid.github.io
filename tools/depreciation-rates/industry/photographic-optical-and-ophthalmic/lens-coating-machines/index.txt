# Optical lens grinding and contact lens manufacturing: Lens coating machines

‘Optical lens grinding and contact lens manufacturing: Lens coating machines’, for an asset used in Photographic, optical and ophthalmic equipment manufacturing, has an effective life of 10 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2004.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Reading this row

In Photographic, optical and ophthalmic equipment manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).
