# Waste gas cleaning and dedusting assets: Dedusting fans

‘Waste gas cleaning and dedusting assets: Dedusting fans’, for an asset used in Iron smelting and steel manufacturing, has an effective life of 10 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2009.

## Reading this row

In Iron smelting and steel manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).
