# Steel strip organic coating assets: Shears

‘Steel strip organic coating assets: Shears’, for an asset used in Iron smelting and steel manufacturing, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2010.

## Reading this row

In Iron smelting and steel manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).
