# Tin-plating mill assets: Acid and alkaline cleaning baths …

‘Tin-plating mill assets: Acid and alkaline cleaning baths …’, for an asset used in Iron smelting and steel manufacturing, has an effective life of 13 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2011.

## Reading this row

In Iron smelting and steel manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2011. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
