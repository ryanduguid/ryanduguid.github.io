# Central production facility assets …: Major carbon steel vessels

‘Central production facility assets …: Major carbon steel vessels’, for an asset used in Oil and gas extraction, has an effective life of 12 1/2 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2002.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Major carbon steel vessels
Under | Oil production assets: / Central production facility assets (excluding FPSOs):
Table | Table A (industry categories), MINING / Oil and gas extraction, ANZSIC 07000
Effective life | 12 1/2 years
Prime cost rate | 8.00% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 16.00% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2002
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

In Oil and gas extraction, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2002. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
