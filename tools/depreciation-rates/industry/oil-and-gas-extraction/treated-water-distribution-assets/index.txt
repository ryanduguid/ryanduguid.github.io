# Water assets: Treated water distribution assets

‘Water assets: Treated water distribution assets’, for an asset used in Oil and gas extraction, has an effective life of 30 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2016.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Treated water distribution assets
Under | Gas production assets: / Coal seam gas extraction assets: / Water assets:
Table | Table A (industry categories), MINING / Oil and gas extraction, ANZSIC 07000
Effective life | 30 years
Prime cost rate | 3.33% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 6.67% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2016
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.
Capped life | Marked #: a capped life is available under section 40-102. See capped lives.

## Reading this row

In Oil and gas extraction, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2016. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
