# Fuel gas systems effective life: 30 years

Source: https://duguid.com.au/tools/depreciation-rates/industry/oil-and-gas-extraction/fuel-gas-systems/

> Fuel gas systems: effective life 30 years, prime cost 3.33%, diminishing value 6.67% if first held from 10 May 2006. Updated 1 July 2002 (Table A, 2025).

# Central production facility assets: Fuel gas systems

‘Central production facility assets: Fuel gas systems’, for an asset used in Oil and gas extraction, has an effective life of 30 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2002.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Fuel gas systems
Under | Gas production assets: / Central production facility assets:
Table | Table A (industry categories), MINING / Oil and gas extraction, ANZSIC 07000
Effective life | 30 years
Prime cost rate | 3.33% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 6.67% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2002
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.
Capped life | Marked #: a capped life is available under section 40-102. See capped lives.

## Reading this row

In Oil and gas extraction, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2002. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
