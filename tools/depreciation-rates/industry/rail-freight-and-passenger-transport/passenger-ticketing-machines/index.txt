# Communication, computer and passenger assets: Passenger ticketing machines

‘Communication, computer and passenger assets: Passenger ticketing machines’, for an asset used in Rail freight and passenger transport services, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2010.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Table | Table A (industry categories), TRANSPORT, POSTAL AND WAREHOUSING / Rail freight and passenger transport services, ANZSIC 47100 to 47200
Effective life | 15 years
Prime cost rate | 6.67% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)

Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

In Rail freight and passenger transport services, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2010. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
