# Infrastructure assets / Electrification assets: Substations (incorporating switchgear and circuit breakers) (Rail freight and passenger transport services)

‘Electrification assets: Substations (incorporating switchgear and circuit breakers)’, for an asset used in Rail freight and passenger transport services, has an effective life of 40 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2002.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 14 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

The determination is the Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Substations (incorporating switchgear and circuit breakers)
Under | Infrastructure assets: / Electrification assets:
Table | Table A (industry categories), TRANSPORT, POSTAL AND WAREHOUSING / Rail freight and passenger transport services, ANZSIC 47100 to 47200
Effective life | 40 years
Prime cost rate | 2.50% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 5.00% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 January 2002
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Rail freight and passenger transport services takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘incorporating’, the items it lists are components of the one asset, not separate assets (paragraph 4 of the guide).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2002. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
