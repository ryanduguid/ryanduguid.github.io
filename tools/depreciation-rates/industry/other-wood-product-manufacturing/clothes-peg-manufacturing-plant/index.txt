# Clothes peg manufacturing plant (wood)

‘Clothes peg manufacturing plant (wood)’, for an asset used in Other wood product manufacturing, has an effective life of 13 1/3 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2001.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Clothes peg manufacturing plant (wood)
Table | Table A (industry categories), MANUFACTURING / Other wood product manufacturing, ANZSIC 14910 to 14990
Effective life | 13 1/3 years
Prime cost rate | 7.50% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 15.00% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 January 2001
Reviewed | No * mark.

## Reading this row

In Other wood product manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 January 2001. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
