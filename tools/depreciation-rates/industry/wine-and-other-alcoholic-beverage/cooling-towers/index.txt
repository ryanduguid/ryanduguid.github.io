# Support assets: Cooling towers (Wine and other alcoholic beverage manufacturing)

‘Support assets: Cooling towers’, for an asset used in Wine and other alcoholic beverage manufacturing, has an effective life of 15 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2008.

## Reading this row

In Wine and other alcoholic beverage manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).
