# Chemical storage tanks: Other

‘Chemical storage tanks: Other’, for an asset used in Basic inorganic chemical manufacturing, has an effective life of 20 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2013.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 26 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Source Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Basic inorganic chemical manufacturing takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2013. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
