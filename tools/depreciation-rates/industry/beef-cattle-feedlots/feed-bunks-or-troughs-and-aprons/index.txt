# Cattle pen infrastructure assets: Feed bunks or troughs and aprons

‘Cattle pen infrastructure assets: Feed bunks or troughs and aprons’, for an asset used in Beef cattle feedlots, has an effective life of 20 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2007.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates. Data from determination F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Feed bunks or troughs and aprons
Under | Cattle pen assets: / Cattle pen infrastructure assets:
Table | Table A (industry categories), AGRICULTURE, FORESTRY AND FISHING / Beef cattle feedlots, ANZSIC 01430
Effective life | 20 years
Prime cost rate | 5.00% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 10.00% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 January 2007
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

In Beef cattle feedlots, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 January 2007. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
