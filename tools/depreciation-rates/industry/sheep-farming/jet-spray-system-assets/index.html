Dipping and spraying assets for parasite control: Jet spray system assets …

‘Dipping and spraying assets for parasite control: Jet spray system assets …’, for an asset used in Sheep farming, has an effective life of 10 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2007.

Reading this row

An asset used in Sheep farming takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2007. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
