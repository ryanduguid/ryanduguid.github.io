‘Plastic safety screens (including sneeze guards)’, for an asset used in Cafes, restaurants, takeaway food services, pubs, taverns, bars and clubs (hospitality), has an effective life of 2 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2022.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Cafes, restaurants, takeaway food services, pubs, taverns, bars and clubs (hospitality) takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2022. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
