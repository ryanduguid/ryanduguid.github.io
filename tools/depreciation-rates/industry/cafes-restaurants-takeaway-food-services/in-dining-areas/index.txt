# Furniture, freestanding, for customer use: In dining areas

‘Furniture, freestanding, for customer use: In dining areas’, for an asset used in Cafes, restaurants, takeaway food services, pubs, taverns, bars and clubs (hospitality), has an effective life of 8 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2005.

## Reading this row

In Cafes, restaurants, takeaway food services, pubs, taverns, bars and clubs (hospitality), this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

Where ‘freestanding’ describes an asset, it means an item designed to be portable or movable, attached to the premises only for temporary stability (paragraph 7 of the guide).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2005. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
