# Refrigeration assets: Ice making machines (Cafes, restaurants, takeaway food services, pubs, taverns, bars and clubs (hospitality))

‘Refrigeration assets: Ice making machines’, for an asset used in Cafes, restaurants, takeaway food services, pubs, taverns, bars and clubs (hospitality), has an effective life of 8 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2014.

## Reading this row

In Cafes, restaurants, takeaway food services, pubs, taverns, bars and clubs (hospitality), this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).
