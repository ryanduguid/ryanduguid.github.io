# Food preparation and service assets: Microwave ovens

‘Food preparation and service assets: Microwave ovens’, for an asset used in Cafes, restaurants, takeaway food services, pubs, taverns, bars and clubs (hospitality), has an effective life of 5 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2005.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 23 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

The determination is the Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Cafes, restaurants, takeaway food services, pubs, taverns, bars and clubs (hospitality) takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2005. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
