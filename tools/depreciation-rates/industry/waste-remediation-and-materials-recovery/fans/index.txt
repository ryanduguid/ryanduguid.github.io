# Materials recovery facility … assets: Fans

‘Materials recovery facility … assets: Fans’, for an asset used in Waste remediation and materials recovery services, has an effective life of 10 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2014.

## Reading this row

In Waste remediation and materials recovery services, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2014. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
