# Eucalyptus oil plant: Tanks

‘Eucalyptus oil plant: Tanks’, for an asset used in Other basic chemical product manufacturing n.e.c., has an effective life of 40 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2001.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 26 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Other basic chemical product manufacturing n.e.c. takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2001. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
