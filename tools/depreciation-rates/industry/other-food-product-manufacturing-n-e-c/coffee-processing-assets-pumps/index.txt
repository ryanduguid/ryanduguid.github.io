# Support assets: Pumps (Other food product manufacturing n.e.c.)

‘Support assets: Pumps’, for an asset used in Other food product manufacturing n.e.c., has an effective life of 20 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2012.

## Reading this row

In Other food product manufacturing n.e.c., this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).
