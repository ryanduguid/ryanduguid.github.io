# Balers

‘Balers’, for an asset used in Solid waste collection services, has an effective life of 7 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2015.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

Table | Table A (industry categories), ELECTRICITY, GAS, WATER AND WASTE SERVICES / Solid waste collection services, ANZSIC 29110

## Reading this row

In Solid waste collection services, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2015. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
