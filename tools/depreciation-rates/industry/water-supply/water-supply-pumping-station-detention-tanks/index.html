Assets used in water supply: Water supply pumping station detention tanks

‘Assets used in water supply: Water supply pumping station detention tanks’, for an asset used in Water supply, has an effective life of 80 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2005.

Reading this row

In Water supply, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).
