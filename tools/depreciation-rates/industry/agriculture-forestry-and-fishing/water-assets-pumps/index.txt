# Drip, micro spray or mini sprinkler systems: Pumps

‘Drip, micro spray or mini sprinkler systems: Pumps’, for an asset used in AGRICULTURE, FORESTRY AND FISHING, has an effective life of 12 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2008.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 26 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Effective life and rates from F2025L01097, registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Pumps
Under | Water assets: / Irrigation assets: / Drip, micro spray or mini sprinkler systems:
Table | Table A (industry categories), AGRICULTURE, FORESTRY AND FISHING, rows for the whole division, ANZSIC 01110 to 05290
Effective life | 12 years
Prime cost rate | 8.33% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 16.67% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2008
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in AGRICULTURE, FORESTRY AND FISHING takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2008. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
