# Production assets: Autoclaves (for terminal sterilisation)

‘Production assets: Autoclaves (for terminal sterilisation)’, for an asset used in Human pharmaceutical and medicinal product manufacturing, has an effective life of 10 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 January 2004.

## Reading this row

In Human pharmaceutical and medicinal product manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).
