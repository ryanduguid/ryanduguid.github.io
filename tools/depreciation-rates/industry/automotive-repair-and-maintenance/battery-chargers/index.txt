# Automotive and heavy vehicle repair and maintenance assets: Battery chargers

‘Automotive and heavy vehicle repair and maintenance assets: Battery chargers’, for an asset used in Automotive repair and maintenance, has an effective life of 5 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2011.

## Reading this row

In Automotive repair and maintenance, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).
