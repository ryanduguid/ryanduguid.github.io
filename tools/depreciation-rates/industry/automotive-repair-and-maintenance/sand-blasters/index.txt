# Automotive and heavy vehicle repair and maintenance assets: Sand blasters

‘Automotive and heavy vehicle repair and maintenance assets: Sand blasters’, for an asset used in Automotive repair and maintenance, has an effective life of 7 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2011.
