# Elevating work platforms …: Boom lifts (including knuckle and telescopic boom lifts)

‘Elevating work platforms …: Boom lifts (including knuckle and telescopic boom lifts)’, for an asset used in Rental and hiring services (except real estate), has an effective life of 15 years (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2015.

## Reading this row

In Rental and hiring services (except real estate), this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2015. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
