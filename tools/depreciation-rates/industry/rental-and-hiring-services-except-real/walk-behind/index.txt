# Concrete trowels: Walk behind (Rental and hiring services (except real estate))

‘Concrete trowels: Walk behind’, for an asset used in Rental and hiring services (except real estate), has an effective life of 5 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2008.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 23 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

The determination is the Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Walk behind
Under | Concreting assets: / Concrete trowels:
Table | Table A (industry categories), RENTAL, HIRING AND REAL ESTATE SERVICES / Rental and hiring services (except real estate), ANZSIC 66110 to 66400
Effective life | 5 years
Prime cost rate | 20.00% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)

Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Rental and hiring services (except real estate) takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2008. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
