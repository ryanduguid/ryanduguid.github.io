# Propagation assets: Seedling and punnet trays, reusable

‘Propagation assets: Seedling and punnet trays, reusable’, for an asset used in Nursery and floriculture production, has an effective life of 3 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2006.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 14 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

The determination is the Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Seedling and punnet trays, reusable
Under | Propagation assets:
Table | Table A (industry categories), AGRICULTURE, FORESTRY AND FISHING / Nursery and floriculture production, ANZSIC 01110 to 01150
Effective life | 3 years
Prime cost rate | 33.33% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 66.67% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2006
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Nursery and floriculture production takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2006. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
