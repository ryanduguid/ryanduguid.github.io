## Reading this row

In Motor vehicle manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

Because the row says ‘incorporating’, the items it lists are components of the one asset, not separate assets (paragraph 4 of the guide).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2011. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
