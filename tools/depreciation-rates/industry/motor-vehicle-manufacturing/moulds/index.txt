# Tooling: Moulds (including fibreglass moulds)

‘Tooling: Moulds (including fibreglass moulds)’, for an asset used in Motor vehicle manufacturing, has an effective life of 7 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2011.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 15 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

The determination is the Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025.

Row | Moulds (including fibreglass moulds)
Under | Bus and truck manufacturing assets (including bus vehicle body assembling assets and truck body manufacturing assets): / Ancillary and support assets: / Tooling:
Table | Table A (industry categories), MANUFACTURING / Motor vehicle manufacturing, ANZSIC 23110
Effective life | 7 years
Prime cost rate | 14.29% (100% ÷ life, section 40-75)
Diminishing value rate | 28.57% (200% ÷ life, section 40-72, for an asset you started to hold on or after 10 May 2006)
Last reviewed and determined | 1 July 2011
Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Motor vehicle manufacturing takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2011. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
