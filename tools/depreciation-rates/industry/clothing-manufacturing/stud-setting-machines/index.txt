# Stud setting machines

‘Stud setting machines’, for an asset used in Clothing manufacturing, has an effective life of 20 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2022.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 27 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

## Effective life and rates

Row | Stud setting machines

## Reading this row

In Clothing manufacturing, this life applies if the asset matches the row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function described (subsection 6(2)). In another industry, use its applicable Table A life. If none applies, use the matching Table B row (subsection 6(3)).

This life was last determined on 1 July 2022. An earlier determination may apply at the time subsection 40-95(2) specifies for your asset. Check which determination applies.
