# Support assets: Audio visual entertainment assets …

‘Support assets: Audio visual entertainment assets …’, for an asset used in Health and fitness centres and gymnasia operation, has an effective life of 4 years under the Commissioner’s 2025 effective life determination (F2025L01097), last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2012.

Published 14 November 2026. Page updated 26 November 2026. Sources checked 13 November 2026.

Reviewed | Yes: the row is marked * as reviewed in the Commissioner’s ongoing review of the determination.

## Reading this row

An asset used in Health and fitness centres and gymnasia operation takes this life if it matches this row exactly or is of a kind that falls within or broadly satisfies the function the row describes (subsection 6(2) of the determination). An asset used in another industry takes the life its own industry gives it, or the Table B row for it where no Table A row applies (subsection 6(3)).

Because the row says ‘including’, each item it lists is treated as a separate asset with this life (paragraph 3 of the guide to Schedule 2).

The life was last reviewed and determined on 1 July 2012. The life that applies is the one in force at the time subsection 40-95(2) fixes for your asset, so an earlier determination may apply to an asset acquired before then: see which determination applies.
