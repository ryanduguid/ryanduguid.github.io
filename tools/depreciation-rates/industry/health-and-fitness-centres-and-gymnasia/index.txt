Each row on this page is quoted from Schedule 2 of the Income Tax Assessment (Effective Life of Depreciating Assets) Determination 2025 (F2025L01097), registered on 15 September 2025, as read on 13 November 2026. All the rows are in the data file, and the depreciation rates page says how it was built and checked.
