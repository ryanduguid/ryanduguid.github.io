## Compare profit with cash flow

Lumbridge Services is a fictional Newcastle maintenance business. Delay one customer receipt by 45 days and inspect the cash gap in its Excel forecast. The quarter’s profit stays the same.

13-week forecast with one receipt paid 45 days late. Weekly closing cash falls to ($25,160) against a $15,000 buffer, then recovers to $67,320 by December, the same as without the delay. Exported from the supplied workbook in Excel 16.0 on 4 November 2026. Capture record.
