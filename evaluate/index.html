$35,957.55 profit, yet a $25,160 cash shortfall

Lumbridge Services is a fictional maintenance business in Newcastle, NSW. Moving one $44,000 customer receipt from 14 October to 28 November 2026 leaves quarterly profit before income tax unchanged at $35,957.55 while the daily cash balance falls to −$25,160.00 on 6 November. All amounts are AUD and every figure is fictional.
