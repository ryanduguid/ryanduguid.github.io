# Evaluations

Explore a synthetic profit-and-cash workbook, then reproduce 3 accounting-tool evaluations at fixed releases using fabricated inputs and hand-recorded expected results.

An evaluation shows what one version did on declared fixtures. It does not prove completeness, suitability, or a professional conclusion.

For identity, source, and release assurance, see the Evidence register. Each evaluated tool is listed in the Tools register.

Last reviewed 14 September 2026.

## Profit of $35,957.55. A cash shortfall of $25,160.

Lumbridge Services is a fictional maintenance business in Newcastle, NSW. This worked example asks whether the owner can pay the next bills if one customer pays late. All businesses, customers, invoices, and figures are fabricated; this is not a client result.

Download the synthetic Excel forecast or download the workbook, source files, and trial guide. Use desktop Excel with automatic calculation. No Xero login, macros, or AI agent is needed.

The forecast covers October to December 2026 in AUD. Moving one $44,000 receipt from 14 October to 28 November changes when cash is available. It leaves the quarter's revenue and profit unchanged.

Same work performed, different receipt dates. Scroll sideways on small screens.

Forecast measure | On time | 45 days late
Quarter revenue | $180,000.00 | $180,000.00
Profit before income tax | $35,957.55 | $35,957.55
October closing cash | $32,040.00 | ($11,960.00)
Lowest daily closing cash | $16,800.00 on 7 October | ($25,160.00) on 6 November
December closing cash | $67,320.00 | $67,320.00

The late case first runs short on 28 October. December cash catches up after the receipt arrives, but that later recovery cannot fund the earlier payments. Keeping the example's assumed $15,000 cash buffer would require $40,160 above the late-case trough.

### The decision to examine

Confirm the customer receipt date and resolve the earlier cash gap before approving discretionary equipment spending. Then check actual collections against the forecast each week. No overdraft or new funding is assumed.

At 31 December, the model still has $12,600 GST, $5,000 PAYG withholding, and $13,200 supplier invoices payable. Its closing bank balance includes cash needed for those obligations.

### Try the workbook

- Open the Model and Cash 13 weeks sheets and find the profit and cash figures.
- Change Assumptions!B2 from 0 to 45 calendar days. Trace invoice OPEN-V and the cash shortfall.
- Restore 0, save a copy, and reopen it. The workbook supports only whole-day receipt delays from 0 to 120; exported CSVs and briefing text stay fixed.

Varrock and Falador are the fictional service lines, named after Old School RuneScape locations. No game knowledge is needed.

### Evidence and limits

This is a maintainer-prepared demonstration. Its calculations have technical verification records; an independent accountant trial has not yet been completed; offer a review of the Lumbridge case. No client outcome, forecast accuracy, or time saving has been measured. The case changes receipt timing only and does not produce a full statutory balance sheet.

Download provenance and workbook checks are in the sample record. Read the source, assumptions, and trial at the captured commit. The Australian pack extends Jeff Brines / Guiderail's openfpa.

## Five-minute accounting cases

Start with a review question and inspect the synthetic inputs, command, and output. These are maintainer-prepared examples, not independent firm evaluations.

### Balanced trial balance, unfinished review

The synthetic July close returns REVIEW: 8 exceptions, none blocked. Three findings explain why the balanced trial balance still needs attention.

Three findings from the supplied case. The 8 exceptions are not 8 accounting errors.

Finding | Evidence | Reviewer action
Creditors reconciliation | Trial balance less supplied subledger: ($250). | Trace the difference and document its cause.
Account mapping | Operating Expenses account 6000 has no review-group mapping. | Confirm the mapping and review owner.
Financial-year reset | The comparison crosses from June to July. | Use a suitable period comparison for P&L YTD movements.

## Manager review gate evaluation

Runs 2 fabricated BAS packs through Workpaper Review Gate, corrects the stated failures, and reproduces READY.

Fabricated input

Two BAS workpaper packs.

Expected result

Exact NOT_READY findings, then READY after correction.

Fixed release

Workpaper Review Gate review-ready-gate/v0.1.3.

Limitation

READY is readiness for a manager, not sign-off.

Evaluation source · Primary source: Australian Taxation Office, Business activity statements

## Xero trial balance integrity evaluation

Runs 3 fabricated CSV fixtures through the movement and year-to-date balance gate without a Xero tenant.

Fabricated input

Balanced and unbalanced trial-balance CSVs.

Expected result

One accepted export and 2 explicit refusals.

Fixed release

Xero trial balance export xero-trial-balance-export/v0.1.6.

Limitation

No tenant credentials, live tenant data, or API availability test.

Evaluation source · Primary source: Xero, Accounting API Reports

## Payday Super timing evidence evaluation

Reproduces 4 evidence-timing outcomes, including at-risk, on-time, and late classifications.

Fabricated input

Four contribution and receipt-evidence fixtures.

Expected result

Exact status and due-date output for each line.

Fixed release

Payday Super Checker payday-super-checker/v0.1.3.

Limitation

Supplied evidence is classified; missing facts are not inferred.

Evaluation source · Primary source: Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992 and Australian Taxation Office, LCR 2026/2
