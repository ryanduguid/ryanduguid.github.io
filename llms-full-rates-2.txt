# What is the GIC rate for October to December 2026? 11.51%

Source: https://duguid.com.au/rates/general-interest-charge/

11.51%

# General interest charge rate

11.51% a year for October to December 2026, which is 0.03153425% a day. It was 11.43% for July to September 2026.

The rate changes each quarter. Section 8AAD of the Taxation Administration Act 1953 sets it at the Reserve Bank’s monthly average yield of 90-day Bank Accepted Bills for a month before the quarter, plus 7 percentage points, and the rate for a day is that annual rate divided by the days in the calendar year.

## How GIC is worked out

GIC for a day is that day’s rate times the unpaid amount and any GIC from earlier days still unpaid, so it compounds daily (section 8AAC). On a $10,000 debt left unpaid for all 92 days of October to December 2026, each day adds 11.51% ÷ 365 of the balance, and the debt grows to $10,294.32: $294.32 of GIC.

The GIC payment plan calculator applies these rates day by day to a debt paid by equal weekly, fortnightly or monthly instalments, against paying it all now.

## Common questions

### What is the GIC rate for October to December 2026?

11.51% a year, or 0.03153425% a day, as the ATO lists it. For July to September 2026 it was 11.43%.

### How is the general interest charge rate set?

Section 8AAD of the Taxation Administration Act 1953 adds 7 percentage points to a base interest rate. The base rate is the Reserve Bank’s monthly average yield of 90-day Bank Accepted Bills for the month the section names (November for January to March, February for April to June, May for July to September and August for October to December), rounded to 2 decimal places. The rate for a day divides the annual rate by the days in the calendar year.

### Does GIC compound?

Yes, daily. Under section 8AAC, the charge for a day is that day’s rate times the unpaid amount and the charge from previous days still unpaid. The ATO says tax debts on a payment plan continue to accrue GIC, which compounds daily.

### Why can a daily rate differ in its last decimal place from the annual rate divided by 365?

The daily rates here are the ATO’s published figures, to 8 decimal places: the annual rate divided by the days in the calendar year, which is 366 in a leap year. In 13 quarters the ATO’s last decimal place is one away from that division rounded to the nearest. The site’s payment plan calculator divides the annual rate itself, as section 8AAD(1) says.

# Transfer duty (stamp duty) rates by state and territory

7% is the top general transfer duty rate for 2026-27: New South Wales premium duty on residential land, on each $100 of value over $3,870,000.

These are the general scales only, before any concession, exemption, first home buyer scheme or foreign purchaser surcharge. Each revenue office decides what is dutiable and the dutiable value.

## What the scales leave out

Concessions, exemptions, first home buyer schemes and the surcharges on foreign purchasers and trusts change the duty payable and are not in these tables. The transfer duty calculator names each jurisdiction’s schemes. The Australian Capital Territory charges lower rates on an eligible owner occupier transaction, and Victoria on a principal place of residence; neither is listed.

## Common questions

### How much transfer duty is payable on a $1,350,000 home in New South Wales?

$55,537 before any concession or surcharge: $52,237 plus $5.50 for every $100, or part of $100, over $1,290,000. That is Revenue NSW’s own worked example.

### Do these rates include first home buyer concessions?

No. They are the general scales. First home buyer schemes, principal place of residence concessions and foreign purchaser surcharges change the duty payable and are not included.

### Which states round the value up to the next $100?

New South Wales, Queensland, Western Australia, South Australia, Tasmania and the Australian Capital Territory charge for every $100 or part of $100, so the excess is rounded up. Victoria, the Northern Territory and the Australian Capital Territory’s band over $1,455,000 work from the exact value.
