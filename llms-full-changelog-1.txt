19 November 2026 | The take-home pay calculator now works the PAYG withheld from each weekly, fortnightly or monthly pay for every year from 2021-22, not only 2026-27, under the withholding schedules the Commissioner made for each year, read on 19 November 2026 from the Federal Register of Legislation. It matches all 288 scale 2 and 3 amounts in each instrument’s sample data. For 2021-22 the refund includes the low and middle income tax offset, which withholding does not allow for. For 2025-26 with a study loan it shows the component under the Schedule 8 for pays before 24 September 2025 beside the one from that date.
19 November 2026 | A new individual tax calculator works out the 6 income measures that the income tests section of a tax return feeds, for a person resident all year in 2024-25 to 2026-27: income for surcharge purposes, Division 293 income, rebate income, repayment income, adjusted taxable income and co-contribution total income. 19 November 2026 | A new business calculator works out one employee’s long service leave under the Act of any of the 8 states and territories, from the service dates and the days that do not count: the leave to take now and when more becomes available, or, when employment ends, whether the reason qualifies for a pro rata payment, the weeks payable less leave already taken, and their value at the weekly pay.
