# Construction WIP under AASB 15: over- or under-billed?

Source: https://duguid.com.au/tools/wip-schedule/

> AASB 15 construction WIP from a contract CSV: cost-to-cost progress, per-contract assets and liabilities, and profit fade. Review aid, not a determination.

# TheWIPTally construction WIP schedule

Package the-wip-tally in the australian-accounting repository. Current release v0.2.0.

TheWIPTally reads one row per unit of account and produces a cost-to-cost work-in-progress schedule: earned revenue after AASB 15 para B19 exclusions, constrained unapproved variations, and a contract asset or contract liability per job. Assets and liabilities are never netted. Review aid, not a determination.

Current release v0.2.0 (19 October 2026). Last reviewed 21 October 2026.

Delivery

Python

Needs

Python 3.14 or later

Boundary

Review aid under AASB 15, not an accounting determination.

Published 25 August 2026. Last reviewed 21 October 2026.

Construction month-end turns on the WIP schedule. The formula is easy. Applying it the same way every month is the work: strip waste from percentage complete without dropping it from margin, book uninstalled materials at cost, constrain unapproved claims and present each contract as an asset or a liability, never a portfolio net.

This tool does the arithmetic. Install the Python engine, point it at a contract CSV and keep the schedule beside the review pack. Recognition over time versus at a point in time, contract combination, claim enforceability and any onerous-contract provision stay with a person. The subcontractor ledger skills own that workflow: they assemble the file and name this engine rather than inventing the numbers.

## What this tool does

- Measures cost-to-cost progress after AASB 15 para B19 exclusions: waste does not accelerate percentage complete; uninstalled materials are recognised at cost with zero margin. Whether uninstalled materials qualify for that treatment depends on the applicable paragraph B19 conditions, so check them against the contract before relying on it; the fixed example below contains none.
- Constrains unapproved variation estimates by the include ratio you supply, flags the excluded slice and never treats an uncertified claim as billing or entitlement.
- Classifies each contract as a contract asset or a contract liability, flags profit fade of 1.00 percentage point or more, and flags a stale estimate to complete after material extra spend.

## Worked example: a $50,000 contract asset

This synthetic example comes from the cost-to-cost underbilling test in the-wip-tally v0.1.2. The test uses a reasonably measurable outcome, with no variations, waste or uninstalled materials. All amounts below exclude GST.

- Transaction price: $1,000,000.
- Costs incurred: $400,000. Estimated cost to complete: $400,000.
- Certified billings: $450,000.

Progress is $400,000 ÷ ($400,000 + $400,000) = 50%. Revenue to date is 50% × $1,000,000 = $500,000. Deducting $450,000 of certified billings leaves a $50,000 contract asset and no contract liability.

A person must confirm that the recognition basis, contract terms and estimates support this calculation. The test’s conditional retention does not change this revenue-minus-billings result; retention classification and final sign-off still need review.

### Reproduce this exact example

## Common questions

### What is a contract asset versus a contract liability?

Revenue to date less certified billings, per contract. Positive is a contract asset. Negative is a contract liability. The engine never offsets one job against another.

### Does wasted cost accelerate percentage complete?

No. Para B19(a) amounts are stripped from the progress measure and kept in estimated cost at completion, so they hit margin instead of pulling revenue forward.

### Are unapproved variations booked in full?

No. Only the constrained slice (unapproved estimate multiplied by the include ratio) enters transaction price. A bare 25 is refused; use 0.25 or 25%.

### Is this an accounting determination?

No. It is a review aid. Recognition over time versus at a point in time, whether a claim is enforceable and any AASB 137 onerous provision stay with the engagement lead.

# Export a Xero trial balance to CSV that balances

# Xero trial balance CSV export tool

Package xero-trial-balance-export in the accounting-review-pipeline repository. Current release v0.1.15.

This tool pulls the trial balance from Xero’s own reporting API and refuses to write the CSV unless both the month’s movement and the year-to-date figures balance. It asks Xero for report reading scope only, so it cannot write anything back to a ledger.

Current release v0.1.15 (12 October 2026). Last reviewed 25 October 2026.

Delivery

Python

Needs

Python 3.11 or later and a free Xero developer app

Boundary

Read-only Xero scope; no ledger writes.

Published 24 August 2026. Last reviewed 25 October 2026.

This tool reads the trial balance from Xero’s reporting API and writes consistent CSV columns for account codes, names, monthly movements and year-to-date balances.

Before anything is written to disk, it runs a balance check: both the current month’s movement and the year-to-date figures must balance, or no file is written. One limit: --date is an as-at date, not a range. Xero’s Trial Balance endpoint accepts only a date, so the Trial Balance by Date Range report, which exists in the Xero interface only, cannot be reproduced this way. The movement columns cover the usual reason for wanting a range: the current month.

## What this tool does

- Pulls a trial balance from Xero’s own reporting API into a tidy CSV that Power BI, Excel or pandas loads without clean-up.
- Refuses to write a file unless both the month’s movement and the year-to-date figures balance.

Related work: XeroAPI accepted my correction to its command-line report flags. It maps --standard-layout and --payments-only to the matching SDK parameters for balance sheet and profit and loss reports. That contribution concerns a separate tool; it does not validate this exporter. Read the accepted-work record.

## Limits

Read-only (accounting.reports.trialbalance.read); this tool cannot write to any ledger.

MIT-licensed utility code, no warranty; outputs feed professional review like any other workpaper input. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Xero.

## Common questions

### Can it export a trial balance for a date range?

No. Xero’s Trial Balance endpoint accepts an as-at date, not a range, so the Trial Balance by Date Range report, which exists in the Xero interface only, cannot be reproduced this way. The movement columns cover the usual reason for wanting a range, which is the current month.

### Can this tool change my Xero data?

No. It requests the accounting.reports.trialbalance.read scope, which is read-only, so it cannot write to any ledger.

### What happens if the trial balance does not balance?

No file is written. The balance check runs before anything reaches disk, and both the month’s movement and the year-to-date figures have to balance for the export to proceed.

# Aus Accounting MCP

Package aus-accounting-mcp in the australian-accounting repository. Current release v0.6.0.

Aus Accounting MCP v0.6.0 (published 20 October 2026) is a local Model Context Protocol server for Australian accounting checks: ATO small business benchmarks, Payday Super timing, limited Division 7A review, GST coding and BAS reconciliation review, and 14 bounded tax and super worksheets. It runs on your machine, ships no ATO document library and lodges nothing. Outputs need human review. The worked example below is pinned to v0.2.8 so its figures stay reproducible.

Current release v0.6.0 (20 October 2026). Last reviewed 21 October 2026.

Delivery

MCP

Needs

Python 3.14 or later and uv

Boundary

Human review required. Optional local library excerpts enter the assistant’s context; Division 7A scope stays limited.

The current published release is aus-accounting-mcp/v0.6.0, verified on GitHub, PyPI and the MCP registry on 20 October 2026. It requires Python 3.14 or later; v0.3.0 remains available for Python 3.11 to 3.13 and v0.2.12 for Python 3.10. Since v0.2.8 it has added contribution cap, account-based pension minimum, study and training support loan repayment, Schedule 8 study loan withholding, annual Medicare levy, low income tax offset and Division 293 worksheets, bringing the total to 14, and pins newer engine releases; v0.3.0 moves Division 7A review to 0.2.0, which finishes a review with an extremely large remaining term instead of hanging. It can also search and read ATO rulings from a point-in-time local copy you configure, and its benchmark comparison adds the labour, rent and motor vehicle ranges from the ATO’s industry pages, which never set the key ratio. Release v0.6.0 adds two GST review tools from the new gst-review engine: one gives each coded bank line a review state against the supplied GST registration dates, and the other compares lodged G1, G2, G3, G10, G11, 1A and 1B figures with the ledger for each period on the cash or accruals basis. Neither classifies a supply or prepares, revises or lodges a BAS. It also pins the Apache-2.0 releases of the Payday Super, benchmark and Division 7A engines, which change no check, comparison or review. The annual Medicare levy worksheet does not adjust payroll withholding. Those changes are outside this guide’s v0.2.8 worked example.

Published 25 August 2026. Last reviewed 21 October 2026. The current release pins ato-benchmark-compare 0.2.2 and australian-tax-calculators 0.2.0. The pinned v0.2.8 example ranks local legislation search results best first and reads long provisions in parts.

The comparisons and capability list below describe reference release v0.2.8. Current release v0.6.0 has 14 worksheets and the additional review tools described above. Use the current README for its complete scope.

## What aus-accounting-mcp does

- Lists and searches the shipped ATO small business benchmark industries.
- Compares the profit and loss buckets you supply to the published ATO ranges. Omitted buckets stay not_supplied; they are not treated as zero.
- Reviews one Payday Super contribution or a related group for one employer, using payday-super-checker. Missing fund receipt evidence remains unresolved.
- Reviews s 109N loan terms and s 109E minimum yearly repayments for one supplied amalgamated loan. Division 7A review is experimental and requires human review.
- The pinned v0.2.8 example calculates 7 experimental tax worksheets through calculate_tax_worksheet, with explicit scope and period checks.
- Searches and reads a configured local Markdown library with file and line citations. This is optional; the package includes no library.
- Emits synthetic SBR fixtures for agent tests, labelled synthetic: true.

Payday Super and ATO benchmarks are delegated to reviewed engines, payday-super-checker and ato-benchmark-compare. The MCP server does not reimplement the law.

The worksheet scope and periods are fixed by the worksheet engine pinned in reference release v0.2.8. Resident basic tax covers full-year resident ordinary income for 2024-25, 2025-26 and 2026-27, before offsets and levies. GST, CGT, depreciation and quarterly SG cover bounded 2025-26 cases; FBT covers the year ended 31 March 2026. The quarterly SG worksheet does not calculate post-June 2026 Payday Super entitlements. PAYG withholding covers one regular weekly, fortnightly or monthly pay from 1 July 2026 on Schedule 1 scale 1, 2, 3, 5 or 6, before tax offsets and Medicare levy adjustments.

Read aus-accounting://scope before confirming worksheet inputs. Keep the exclusions returned with each result. The release instructions also explain optional local library setup. Any retrieved excerpts enter the calling assistant’s context.

## Primary sources

Payday Super timing draws on the Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992 and Australian Taxation Office, LCR 2026/2. Benchmark comparisons use the ATO’s small business benchmarks. The limited Division 7A review applies sections 109N and 109E of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936.

Each engine’s source review and release record state what was checked. Verify current sources and the supplied facts before using an output.

## Common questions

### What is aus-accounting-mcp?

A local Model Context Protocol server for ATO benchmark comparison, Payday Super timing, limited Division 7A review and optional search of a configured Markdown library. This guide’s v0.2.8 reference release has 7 bounded tax worksheets; published v0.6.0 has 14 and adds GST coding and BAS reconciliation review. Outputs need human review.

### Is aus-accounting-mcp a hosted ATO document library?

No. Aus Accounting MCP is a separate project from ato-mcp. It ships no ATO document library and does not fetch rulings or legislation from the web. You can configure a local Markdown folder for search and reading with file and line citations. Retrieved excerpts enter the calling assistant’s context.

### Does it calculate Division 7A repayments?

It reviews s 109E minimum yearly repayments for one supplied amalgamated loan. This is experimental and requires human review. It refuses loan formation, s 109R repayment classification, unpaid present entitlements and distributable surplus.

### Does it lodge with the ATO?

No. It does not lodge, declare or replace a registered agent. SBR payloads it can emit are synthetic test fixtures.

### When is a Payday Super contribution on time?

Since 1 July 2026, the fund must receive it within 7 business days of payday, or 20 business days for a first contribution to a new fund. Sending money to a clearing house does not stop the clock.

# Workpaper Review Gate

Package review-ready-gate in the accounting-review-pipeline repository. Current release v0.3.0.

Current release review-ready-gate v0.3.0 (14 October 2026). The worked example below is pinned to v0.1.3. This tool inspects a folder of workpapers and answers READY, NOT_READY or BLOCKED. A missing GST control export, an unsigned self-review or a trial balance that does not balance never reaches the manager.

Current release v0.3.0 (14 October 2026). Last reviewed 21 October 2026.

Delivery

## Inspect a fictional BAS pack

Release review-ready-gate/v0.1.3 returns NOT_READY for its incomplete pack and READY with no findings for its corrected pack. Both runs were reproduced on 17 October 2026 using the unchanged fictional fixtures.

Three findings in the incomplete pack’s generated findings.csv.

Supplied evidence | Finding code | Status
gst_control_gl.csv is absent. | MISSING_ARTEFACT | NOT_READY
pack_complete, tie_outs_done and self_reviewed are false in self_review.json. | SELF_REVIEW_INCOMPLETE | NOT_READY

Download the generated findings.csv, including the reasons, repeat flag and reviewer actions. Inspect the incomplete input pack and corrected input pack at that release.

The corrected pack’s generated cover sheet records Overall status: READY and Findings: 0 total; 0 blocked; 0 not ready; 0 repeats. A human still decides whether the file is acceptable. The fixed-release evaluation gives both reproduction commands.

Preparation got cheaper and review did not. Offshore teams, juniors and AI agents can produce a file faster than a manager can reconstruct it, so managers spend their review time finding the missing tie-out, the open item still sitting in email and the same error as last period.

Point the CLI at a pack directory. It checks that the required artefacts are present, that the preparer has certified the pack, that blocking open items are cleared and that the trial balance balances. For a BAS pack, it also checks that 1A less 1B ties to the GST control movement. A READY pack may enter manager review. The tool still does not decide that the work is correct.

## What this tool does

- Gates BAS, month-end and year-end packs before they reach a reviewer.
- Writes a cover sheet, a findings CSV and a hashed JSON pack a manager can archive.
- Flags findings that were still OPEN on the prior pack as repeats.

## Limits

Local files only. No Xero connection, no lodgement, no LLM, no period lock. A READY result is not sign-off.

MIT-licensed utility code, no warranty. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the ATO or Xero. Not tax advice.

## Get it

Run the two fictional BAS packs and compare the expected findings. The fixed-release evaluation shows the failures to correct before READY, with no client files or Xero connection.

Needs Python 3.10 or later. A command-line tool for whoever runs Python at your firm.

git clone https://github.com/ryanduguid/accounting-review-pipeline.git
git -C accounting-review-pipeline checkout review-ready-gate/v0.1.3
cd accounting-review-pipeline/packages/review-ready-gate
python -m pip install -e ".[dev]"
review-ready gate --profile bas --pack examples/bas-ready --output ../../../review-ready-demo/bas-ready
```

The output goes outside the clone on purpose. A readiness pack names a client’s file and its open findings, so it belongs outside version control rather than behind an ignore entry the next commit can waive. Releases v0.1.7 to v0.1.9 refuse an output inside a version control checkout. The path above leaves the clone, which is what you want when you run these commands from an ordinary directory. If you are already inside another checkout, point --output at a directory outside that one instead.

## Common questions

### Is READY the same as approved?

No. READY means no configured gate tripped on the files that were present. A human still decides whether the file can be signed or lodged.

### Does this replace monthly close review?

No. Monthly Close Controls still produces the exception pack once a trial balance is allowed onto the desk. This tool sits upstream of that.

No. It reads local files and writes a readiness pack. It cannot post, pay, lodge or lock a period.

## Worked example: balanced trial balance, unfinished review

The fictional example compares 31 July 2026 with 30 June 2026. With thresholds of $10,000 and 10%, and a reconciliation tolerance of $0.01, the result is REVIEW: 8 exceptions, none blocked, and 6 draft client queries.

### From supplied balances to an exception

Selected values for account 200, Trade Creditors. Amounts are AUD; a credit balance is negative.

File | Field | Value
current_trial_balance.csv | YTDCredit | 65000.00
subledger_balances.csv | SubledgerBalance | -64750.00
exceptions.csv | difference | -250.00

The engine compares -65000.00 with -64750.00 and writes subledger_reconciliation,REVIEW. Download the generated exceptions.csv with all 8 exceptions, or inspect the complete fictional input files at the same commit. Reproduced on 17 October 2026.

Exception | Result | Reviewer action
Creditors reconciliation | Trial balance less supplied subledger: −$250. | Trace the reconciling items and document their cause.
Account mapping | Operating Expenses, account 6000, has no supplied mapping. | Confirm the account’s review group.
Financial year reset | The comparison crosses 1 July. | Resolve the reset before treating profit and loss YTD differences as ordinary monthly movements.

Five threshold movements make up the remaining exceptions. The 8 exceptions are not 8 accounting errors. The review acknowledgement leaves the result at REVIEW.

The captured manager case study explains the selected exceptions. The command below reproduces the 4-file output at that same commit. All inputs are fictional.

## Inputs

Start with canonical CSVs written by the Xero trial balance exporter. The example uses these inputs:

- Current and prior trial balances, required: one organisation and one report date in each file. Both must name the same organisation, with the prior date earlier than the current date.
- Account mapping, optional: a CSV assigning accounts to review groups. With a supplied file, missing mappings are flagged for review. Omit it and the mapping control is not run.
- Subledger balances, optional: supplied balances to compare with the trial balance. Omit them and subledger reconciliation is not run.
- Review note, optional: a human acknowledgement. It does not clear exceptions or approve the close.

Set the absolute and percentage materiality thresholds and reconciliation tolerance for the review. Choose an output directory outside version control. The pinned source documentation defines the accepted files and controls.

## Outputs and status

- close-summary.md: overall status, thresholds, source hashes and the exception table.
- exceptions.csv: account-level differences, reasons and suggested reviewer actions for Excel or Power BI.
- client-queries.csv: draft questions for exceptions a client can help resolve. A preparer must review them before sending anything.
- close-review-pack.json: structured results and evidence for the run, including any supplied review acknowledgement.

PASS means the configured checks passed on the supplied files. REVIEW or BLOCKED requires attention and returns exit code 2. Malformed inputs, invalid configuration or an output path the tool refuses return exit code 1. A human decides whether the close is acceptable.

## Run the example

Needs Git and Python 3.10 or later. The pinned source command uses Python’s standard library. It needs no Xero account, API access or client files.

```
git clone https://github.com/ryanduguid/accounting-review-pipeline.git
cd accounting-review-pipeline
git checkout ae88966fc416f6f2ad618652779c50d0e348949d
cd packages/monthly-close-control-plane
```

python -m closecontrol.cli review --current examples/current_trial_balance.csv --prior examples/prior_trial_balance.csv --mapping examples/account_mapping.csv --subledger examples/subledger_balances.csv --absolute-threshold 10000 --percentage-threshold 0.10 --reconciliation-tolerance 0.01 --review-note examples/review_note.json --output ../../../close-control-demo
```

Expect exit code 2 and REVIEW; 8 exception(s); 6 client query(ies) drafted. The output directory is beside the checkout. Keep review packs outside version control; the command refuses an output directory inside a checkout.

## Limits

A balanced trial balance can still contain omissions, misclassifications or unsupported transactions. These checks use the files, mappings and thresholds you supply. They do not establish that the source records are complete or that an accounting treatment is correct.

The tool cannot post journals, make payments, lodge returns, lock a period or approve a close. It does not send the draft client queries. A reviewer must investigate the exceptions and record the decision through the firm’s workpaper process.

## Common questions

### Does Monthly Close Controls connect to Xero?

No. It reads local CSV files in the canonical Xero trial balance export format. Use the Xero trial balance exporter separately to obtain those files.

### Can I open the exception report in Excel?

Yes. Import exceptions.csv to filter the account-level findings, differences and reviewer actions. The tool also writes a Markdown summary and a JSON evidence pack.

### Does PASS approve the month-end close?

No. PASS means the configured checks passed on the supplied inputs. A human still reviews completeness, accounting treatment and the evidence before approving a close.

### What will my income tax bill be?

For 2026-27, a resident pays no tax on the first $18,200, 15% from $18,201 to $45,000, 30% to $135,000, 37% to $190,000 and 45% above that, plus the 2% Medicare levy. Start with taxable income for the correct income year, then account for levies, offsets, study loans and tax already withheld. A basic tax bracket result alone is incomplete.

- Gather income statements, investment statements, deductible expenses and PAYG credits.
- Use the ATO return estimate and reconcile each adjustment before treating it as a budget.

The linked experimental income tax worksheet excludes levies and offsets; check its supported years.

2.

### Which expenses reduce my taxable income?

To claim a work-related expense, you must have paid it yourself without reimbursement, it must directly relate to earning your income, and you need a record to prove it, usually a receipt. From 2026-27, eligible taxpayers can claim a standard deduction of up to $1,000 for work-related expenses without records; it is reduced by any work-related expenses they claim that it covers. An expense needs a connection to assessable income and the evidence required for that type of claim. Private spending and reimbursed amounts need separate treatment.

- List each expense, its purpose, date, amount and reimbursement.
- Record your work use calculation and check the ATO category before claiming.

A receipt proves spending, but does not by itself establish deductibility.

### What steps are involved in completing a return?

Most pre-fill data from employers, banks, health funds and government agencies is finalised by the end of July; check that it includes all your income before you lodge. If you lodge your own return, it is due by 31 October; to use a registered tax agent’s later dates, engage them before then. Collect the year’s records, reconcile them with pre-fill information, calculate supported claims, then review and lodge through an authorised channel.

- Check income statements are ready and account for income missing from pre-fill.
- Keep a copy of the reviewed return, declaration and lodgement receipt.

The checklist prepares your records; lodgement happens with the ATO or your registered agent.

### Where can I obtain or recover my TFN?

Applying for a TFN is free, online or on paper depending on your residency and circumstances. If you have a TFN but can’t remember it, find it in ATO online services through myGov, or phone the ATO. Use the ATO’s TFN application or lost-TFN route. The right application process depends on residency and circumstances.

- If you already have a TFN, use the ATO recovery instructions before applying again.
- Complete any identity checks directly with the official service.

Do not put a TFN or identity documents into this website or a public issue.

### What dates govern the annual tax cycle?

The income year runs from 1 July to 30 June. A return you lodge yourself is due by 31 October, quarterly BAS is due on 28 October, 28 February, 28 April and 28 July, and STP finalisation is due by 14 July. Build a calendar from the dates that apply to your return, activity statements, payroll and entity obligations.

- Record the period, lodgement date, payment date and responsible person for each obligation.
- Confirm the dates in your ATO account or with your registered agent; record any approved extension.

An agent’s program or a particular account can have different dates from a general calendar.

### Which methods can I use to settle my tax bill?

You can pay the ATO by BPAY, direct deposit, debit card, Government EasyPay, in person at Australia Post or through an international money remitter. Credit card payments are accepted only until 30 November 2026. Use the ATO payment instructions for the relevant account and payment reference.

- Check the amount owing and available payment methods in the official service.
- Allow for processing time and keep the receipt; reconcile the payment when it appears.

7.

### Which earnings are taxable?

You must declare employment income, government payments and allowances, investment income such as interest, dividends, rent and capital gains, business, partnership and trust income, super pensions and annuities, and foreign income. Some compensation and insurance payments, scholarships, prizes and awards are also taxable. Check each source of income against the ATO’s categories. Amounts missing from pre-fill still need review.

- Reconcile employment, business, investment, government and foreign income records.
- List unusual receipts separately and record why they are included or excluded.

A bank deposit is not automatically taxable income, and taxable income is not limited to deposits.

### How long will the tax return process take?

Most returns lodged online are processed within 12 business days. For a paper return, most refunds are issued within 50 business days. Separate your preparation time from the ATO’s processing time. Missing records, amendments and reviews can extend the overall process.

- Complete the record checklist before setting a preparation deadline.
- After lodgement, use the official progress service and respond to any information request.

No refund date or processing outcome is guaranteed here.

### Where can I submit a completed return?

You can lodge online with myTax, through a registered tax agent or on paper by mail. Registered tax agents are the only people who can charge a fee to prepare and lodge your return. Individuals can use the lodgement options on the ATO’s tax return page, including myTax where eligible, a registered agent or paper.

- Choose the route for your circumstances and confirm the income year.
- Review the declaration and save the lodgement receipt after submission.

The site supplies preparation guidance and does not submit returns.

### Which tax rules changed from 1 July 2026?

From 1 July 2026, the 16% personal tax rate fell to 15%, Payday Super generally requires contributions to reach the fund within 7 business days of payday, the $20,000 instant asset write-off became permanent for small businesses and the cents per kilometre rate rose to 91 cents. Most changes start on 1 July, so compare 2025-26 with 2026-27 rather than calendar years.

- Use the ATO 'Before you prepare your tax return' guidance for each year.
- Record each changed rate or rule, effective date, source and affected calculation in a change log.

An announcement is not evidence that a measure applied to your transaction. This is a review procedure, not an exhaustive legislative history.
