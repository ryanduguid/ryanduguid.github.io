# Open-source accounting tools for Australian accountants.

Find gaps in workpaper packs, explore cash-flow shortfalls, and check calculations with the working visible.

A personal open-source project by Ryan Duguid, an accountant in Newcastle. Not an accounting practice: every output is a review aid for a qualified professional.

Last reviewed 22 September 2026.

## Profit does not pay the next bill.

Lumbridge Services is a fictional Newcastle maintenance business. Delay one customer receipt by 45 days and inspect the cash gap in its Excel forecast. The quarter's profit stays the same.

## Test it with fabricated data first.

Use the public sample files, all fabricated data, to see what a tool accepts, refuses, and explains before it enters a firm workflow.

A receipt date alone does not establish the amount received. Checker 0.1.7 leaves a timely receipt without an amount unknown; older 0.1.6 assumes full receipt. Check the version-specific rules.

The Payday Super evidence-pack workflow combines a report, exceptions and human decision log. It is available in published checker 0.1.4 and MCP 0.2.2. The evaluation retains its original development snapshot.
